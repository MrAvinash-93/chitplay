About ChitPlay

CHITPLAY is a free 3D version of the four-player paper chit game, made for every age: children, teenagers, parents and grandparents. Four players sit around a mat, draw folded chits and bluff, and the investigator has to catch the thief. You can play with three computer players, play with friends on nearby phones, or just watch.

Who makes ChitPlay

ChitPlay is designed and developed by Mr Avinash as an independent project, to keep a game many of us played on paper as children alive for anyone with a browser.

Questions, ideas, bug reports or business enquiries: chitplaygame@gmail.com. See the contact page for more.

How it's made

The game runs on your device: the 3D room, the characters, their voices (your browser's built-in speech), your profile and your saved match. There are no accounts and no downloads, and it works on phones, tablets and computers, even offline once it's been opened.

How ChitPlay stays free

ChitPlay is free to play. We use Google Analytics to understand which games people enjoy, and the site may show advertising to cover its costs. Our Privacy Policy explains both.
