About ChitPlay

ChitPlay is a free 3D living room full of indoor games that you play in your browser. It started with one game, the four-player paper chit game known as Raja Rani Chor Police, and it now has more than thirty: board games, card games, party games and arcade games, all at one table, for players of every age.

You can play every game as a guest with no sign-up: against computer players, with people online, with friends on their own phones nearby (one shares a code, the others join), or on one phone handed round the table.

Who makes ChitPlay

ChitPlay is designed and developed by Mr Avinash, an independent developer from India. The aim is simple: keep the games people play at home, at school and with family easy to reach for anyone with a phone or a computer, without downloads, accounts or real money.

Questions, ideas, bug reports or business enquiries: chitplaygame@gmail.com. See the contact page for more.

The games

  • The chit game: Raja Rani Chor Police (Cops and Robbers), with the rules and role names of fourteen countries, from Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi to Mafia and Räuber und Gendarm.
  • Arcade games on the room’s TV: Flying Chit, Tunnel Race, Hoop Flick, Ghost Hostel, Pin Spin, Ship Fight, Bull Fight, Road Dash, Backyard Rally, Hungry Snake.
  • Board games: Ludo, Carrom, Chess, Snakes and Ladders, Checkers, Four in a Row, Reversi, Mancala, Nine Men’s Morris.
  • Card games: Color Clash, Call Break, Crazy Eights.
  • Puzzle games: Tic-Tac-Toe, Dots and Boxes, Memory Match.
  • Dice games: Yacht, Pig.
  • Party games: Who Is the Spy?, Werewolf Night, Truth or Dare, Riddle Chits, Majority Vote, Consequences.

Every game has its own page with the full rules, scoring, tips and answers to common questions. The games list has them all.

How it's made

Everything runs on your device: the 3D room, the characters, the computer players, your profile and your saved games. There are no accounts and no downloads, and it works on phones, tablets and computers, even offline once it has been opened. Games with friends nearby connect phone to phone; online games share only the moves of the game.

The arcade games, the room and the characters are our own designs. The traditional games follow their usual rules, and where a game has regional versions, its page says which one ChitPlay plays.

Fair play

  • No real money, betting or prizes anywhere. Points and coins have no value outside the game.
  • No pop-ups while you play: rewards and messages wait until a game is over.
  • Chat with other players is limited to emojis and short ready-made lines.

How ChitPlay stays free

ChitPlay is free to play. We use Google Analytics to understand which games people enjoy, and the information pages may show advertising to cover the costs of running the site. Our Privacy Policy explains both.
