Fun party games

Updated: 2026-11-14

A party game should be easy to explain, quick to start and loud enough to fill a room. The best ones put everyone in the spotlight for a moment, then move on before anyone gets bored.

Here are party games that work for birthdays, get-togethers and festivals, for small groups and big ones, in person or on everyone's phone.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Dumb Charades

    One player acts out a film, book or song title without speaking while their team guesses. Agree on signs for "film", "song" and the number of words first.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

  • Two Truths and a Lie

    Each player says three things about themselves; one is made up. The others vote on which one is the lie.

    👥 3+ players · You need: nothing

  • Antakshari (song chain)

    Sing a few lines of any song; the next team must start a new song with the last letter or sound you ended on. A team that cannot think of one loses the round.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

  • Hot and Cold

    Hide a small object while one player waits outside. When they come back, everyone says "warmer" or "colder" as they get nearer or further.

    👥 3+ players · You need: one small object

Tips

  • Start with a quick, simple game while people are still arriving.
  • Hidden-role games such as Who Is the Spy get better with every round.
  • Keep the forfeits friendly so nobody minds losing.

Questions

What are good party games for adults?

Who Is the Spy, Werewolf, Two Truths and a Lie, Majority Vote and Dumb Charades all work well for adult groups.

What party games can we play on our phones?

On ChitPlay a whole group can play Who Is the Spy, Werewolf, Truth or Dare and Majority Vote, each person on their own phone.
