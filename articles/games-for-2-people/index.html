Games for 2 people

Updated: 2026-11-14

Two players is the perfect number for a real contest: nobody to hide behind and every move counts. Whether you want a long think or a quick laugh, there is a two-player game for it.

These are the best games for two, from timeless strategy to fast duels, to play face to face or online.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Thumb War

    Lock fingers with your opponent and try to pin their thumb down for a count of three.

    👥 2 players · You need: nothing

  • Rock, Paper, Scissors

    Count to three and show a hand sign: rock blunts scissors, scissors cut paper, paper wraps rock. Play best of five.

    👥 2 players · You need: nothing

  • Twenty Questions

    One player thinks of a person, animal or object. The others ask up to twenty yes-or-no questions to work out what it is.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

Tips

  • Swap who goes first each game, since the first move is often an advantage.
  • Play best of three to make up for a lucky win.
  • Try a quick game after a long one to keep things fresh.

Questions

What is the best game for two people?

Chess, Carrom and Checkers are the great classics; for something quicker, try Four in a Row or Ship Fight on ChitPlay.

Can two people play online on different phones?

Yes. On ChitPlay one player makes a room and the other joins with the code or link, from anywhere.
