Games to play at home

Updated: 2026-11-14

A good game at home turns an ordinary evening into one people remember. You do not need a cupboard full of boxes: some of the best games need nothing but the people in the room, and others are a few taps away on a phone.

Below are games that work in a living room, on a rainy afternoon or after dinner, for one person or a whole household. Pick by how many people you have and how much energy is left.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Dumb Charades

    One player acts out a film, book or song title without speaking while their team guesses. Agree on signs for "film", "song" and the number of words first.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

  • Antakshari (song chain)

    Sing a few lines of any song; the next team must start a new song with the last letter or sound you ended on. A team that cannot think of one loses the round.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

  • Hot and Cold

    Hide a small object while one player waits outside. When they come back, everyone says "warmer" or "colder" as they get nearer or further.

    👥 3+ players · You need: one small object

  • Story Chain

    One person starts a story with a sentence; each player adds the next one. The sillier the turns, the better the story.

    👥 3+ players · You need: nothing

Tips

  • Mix one active game with one quiet one, so everyone gets a turn to shine.
  • Keep rounds short: five or ten minutes keeps everyone interested.
  • Let the youngest or newest player choose the first game.

Questions

What can I play at home with no board games?

Charades, I Spy, Twenty Questions and Story Chain need nothing at all. On a phone or computer you can also play Ludo, Carrom and the chit game free on ChitPlay.

What games can I play at home alone?

Puzzles and score-chasing games: Unblock Puzzle, Word Guess and Memory Match on ChitPlay, or any board game against the computer.
