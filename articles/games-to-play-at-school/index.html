Games to play at school

Updated: 2026-11-14

Some of the best games ever played were invented on the back page of a school notebook. They are quick, they need almost nothing, and they can be put away in a second when the teacher walks in.

These games suit a free period, a break or a classroom activity. Many also help with spelling, numbers or memory, so teachers can use them too.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Name, Place, Animal, Thing

    Pick a letter. Everyone writes a name, a place, an animal and a thing starting with it, as fast as possible. Unique answers score more.

    👥 2+ players · You need: paper and pens

  • Hangman

    One player draws dashes for a secret word. The others guess letters; every wrong letter adds a line to the drawing. Guess the word before it is finished.

    👥 2+ players · You need: paper and a pen

  • Word Chain

    Say a word; the next player says a word that starts with its last letter. No repeats, and you have five seconds.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

  • Rock, Paper, Scissors

    Count to three and show a hand sign: rock blunts scissors, scissors cut paper, paper wraps rock. Play best of five.

    👥 2 players · You need: nothing

Tips

  • Paper games such as Dots and Boxes and Hangman fit on one notebook page.
  • For a whole class, split into teams and keep the score on the board.
  • Brain games like Number Memory make a good warm-up before a lesson.

Questions

What games can you play in class with paper?

The four-chit game (Raja Rani Chor Police), Dots and Boxes, Tic-Tac-Toe, Hangman, Bingo and Name, Place, Animal, Thing all need just paper and a pen.

Are there educational games to play at school?

Yes. Word Guess and Fast Typing practise English words, Number Memory trains memory, and Name, Place, Animal, Thing builds vocabulary.
