Games to play with friends

Updated: 2026-11-14

The best games with friends are the ones that get people talking, laughing and accusing each other. Bluffing games, quick contests and team games all work, in the same room or with everyone on their own phone.

Here are games that suit a group of friends, from two to ten people, with ideas for when you are together and for when you are not.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Two Truths and a Lie

    Each player says three things about themselves; one is made up. The others vote on which one is the lie.

    👥 3+ players · You need: nothing

  • Would You Rather

    Ask a choice between two funny or tricky options, like "Would you rather speak only in songs or only in rhymes?", and everyone explains their answer.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

  • Dumb Charades

    One player acts out a film, book or song title without speaking while their team guesses. Agree on signs for "film", "song" and the number of words first.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

Tips

  • Bluffing games are best when everyone knows each other: old habits become clues.
  • Agree on a harmless forfeit for last place before you start.
  • For online play, use a voice or video call alongside the game.

Questions

On ChitPlay you can play Raja Rani Chor Police, Who Is the Spy, Ludo, Call Break, Bingo and more, free in the browser. One person makes a room and shares a code or link.

What is a good game for a big group of friends?

Who Is the Spy and Werewolf work for five to ten people; Dumb Charades works for any number split into two teams.
