Games you can play without anything

Updated: 2026-11-14

Stuck in a queue, on a long journey or at home with nothing to play with? You do not need a single thing. Your voice, your hands and a bit of imagination are enough for hours of games.

Below are games that need no equipment at all, and a few free ones on ChitPlay for when all you have is a phone.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Rock, Paper, Scissors

    Count to three and show a hand sign: rock blunts scissors, scissors cut paper, paper wraps rock. Play best of five.

    👥 2 players · You need: nothing

  • Twenty Questions

    One player thinks of a person, animal or object. The others ask up to twenty yes-or-no questions to work out what it is.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

  • I Spy

    Say "I spy with my little eye something beginning with…" and a letter. Everyone looks around and guesses the object. Whoever guesses right picks the next one.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

  • Word Chain

    Say a word; the next player says a word that starts with its last letter. No repeats, and you have five seconds.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

  • Would You Rather

    Ask a choice between two funny or tricky options, like "Would you rather speak only in songs or only in rhymes?", and everyone explains their answer.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

  • Simon Says

    The leader gives actions. Do them only when the order starts with "Simon says"; anyone who moves without it is out.

    👥 3+ players · You need: nothing

  • Thumb War

    Lock fingers with your opponent and try to pin their thumb down for a count of three.

    👥 2 players · You need: nothing

  • Story Chain

    One person starts a story with a sentence; each player adds the next one. The sillier the turns, the better the story.

    👥 3+ players · You need: nothing

Tips

  • Word and guessing games are perfect for car or train journeys.
  • Make the rules stricter as everyone gets better: shorter time, harder letters.
  • If someone has a phone, ChitPlay games need no download and no sign-up.

Questions

What games can you play with nothing?

Rock, Paper, Scissors, Twenty Questions, I Spy, Word Chain, Would You Rather, Simon Says and Thumb War all need nothing at all.

What can two people play without anything?

Twenty Questions, Word Chain, Rock, Paper, Scissors and Thumb War work well for two.
