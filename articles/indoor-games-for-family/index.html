Indoor games for the whole family

Updated: 2026-11-14

The best family games are the ones where a grandparent and a teenager have the same chance of winning. Luck, bluffing and silly fun level the table in a way that pure skill cannot.

These indoor games suit family evenings, festivals and holidays, with mixed ages around the same table, or around the same phone.

Classic games you can play anywhere

  • Antakshari (song chain)

    Sing a few lines of any song; the next team must start a new song with the last letter or sound you ended on. A team that cannot think of one loses the round.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

  • Dumb Charades

    One player acts out a film, book or song title without speaking while their team guesses. Agree on signs for "film", "song" and the number of words first.

    👥 4+ players · You need: nothing

  • Simon Says

    The leader gives actions. Do them only when the order starts with "Simon says"; anyone who moves without it is out.

    👥 3+ players · You need: nothing

  • I Spy

    Say "I spy with my little eye something beginning with…" and a letter. Everyone looks around and guesses the object. Whoever guesses right picks the next one.

    👥 2+ players · You need: nothing

Tips

  • Choose games where luck plays a part, so younger players can win too.
  • Team up the youngest with the oldest.
  • Keep a family score sheet and crown a champion at the end of the holiday.

Questions

What is the best indoor game for the whole family?

Ludo, Snakes and Ladders and the chit game Raja Rani Chor Police are loved by every age, and all three are free on ChitPlay. Without any equipment, Antakshari and Dumb Charades are family favourites.

How do we play together when family lives far apart?

Open the same game on ChitPlay, make a room and share the link or code. Everyone plays from their own phone.
