What to write to us about

  • Bugs: tell us the game you were playing, your phone or computer, and your browser.
  • Game ideas: a version of the game from your country or a rule we've got wrong.
  • Privacy requests: questions about your data. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Copyright concerns: identify the content and explain your rights in it.
  • Advertising and business enquiries.

Before you write

  • How do I play a game? Every game has a page with its rules: see all games or the how-to-play guide.
  • A friend can't join my game nearby. Both phones need to be online. Check the code and try again: on a slow network the second try usually works.
  • My progress is gone. ChitPlay saves on your device. Clearing your browser's data or using a private window removes it.
  • Is there an app? You can add ChitPlay to your home screen from the browser menu. It then opens like an app, even offline.

ChitPlay is run by one independent developer, so a reply may take a few days. We read every message.
