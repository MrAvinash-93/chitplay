Online play and chat

Online and nearby games connect you with other players. ChitPlay limits chat to emojis and short ready-made lines, and doesn't ask for or show personal details. Player names are chosen by players; if you see an offensive one, tell us.

Take breaks, and look away from the screen now and then. The arcade games have moving pictures and quick flashes; if bright flashes affect you, play the board, card and party games instead.
