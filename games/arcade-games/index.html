Arcade games: 3D, on the room’s TV

Ten arcade games of our own, played on the big TV in the ChitPlay room: steer a paper chit through bangles, fly down a turning tunnel, dodge cows in an auto-rickshaw, flick a ball through moving hoops, survive a night in a haunted hostel and more.

Each one has levels that bring something new, from wind on the court to spinning walls, and a pause menu when you need a break. Play alone for a high score, or race up to three friends on one screen or on their own phones.

10 arcade games to play

  • Flying Chit 👥 1–4 players · ⏱ 1–5 minutes
    Steer a winged chit through bangles in an endless classroom. Chain them for combos.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Tunnel Race 👥 1–4 players · ⏱ 1–5 minutes
    A paper plane in an endless tunnel. Steer round the walls and grab the gems.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Hoop Flick 👥 1–2 players · ⏱ 1–5 minutes
    Pull back, let go, and drop the ball through the next hoop. Swish them in a row for combos.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Ghost Hostel 👥 1–4 players · ⏱ 3 minutes
    One night in a haunted hostel. Sleep for coins, build up your door and fans, and last till sunrise.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Pin Spin 👥 1–4 players · ⏱ 1–5 minutes
    Push pins into a spinning corkboard without touching the others. Fill board after board.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Ship Fight 👥 2 players · ⏱ 2–5 minutes
    Two ships, cannons firing non-stop. Dodge, line up your shots and sink your friend.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Bull Fight 👥 2 players · ⏱ 2–5 minutes
    Two bulls, one sand ring. Charge, lock horns and push the other one out.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Road Dash 👥 1–4 players · ⏱ 1–5 minutes
    An auto-rickshaw on a road that never ends. Dodge cows and buses, grab the coins.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Backyard Rally 👥 2 players · ⏱ 2–4 minutes
    Bat and ball in the backyard. The rally keeps getting faster. First to seven.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Hungry Snake 👥 1–4 players · ⏱ 1–5 minutes
    Eat the snacks, grow longer, and don’t bite anything. Faster with every bite.

Questions

Which arcade game is best to start with?

Flying Chit and Road Dash: one finger to steer, and the first levels are gentle.

Do the arcade games work on phones?

Yes. Drag to steer on a phone; on a computer use the keys or a gamepad. Turn the phone sideways for a bigger picture.
