Backyard Rally is the oldest video game idea there is, set in the backyard: two bats, one tennis ball, and a rally that speeds up with every hit. Where the ball meets your bat decides the angle it leaves at.

Ways to play Backyard Rally

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Move your bat up and down.
  2. Hit the ball back. Each hit makes it a little faster.
  3. The edge of the bat sends the ball off at a steeper angle.
  4. If the ball gets past your bat, the other player scores.

Scoring

One point each time the ball gets past the other player.

Who wins

The first to seven points wins.

Tips

  • Use the edge of the bat to send the ball where the other player isn't.
  • Come back to the middle after every hit.

Questions

Can I play alone?

Yes, against a computer player, who gets flustered as the rally speeds up.

What controls can I use?

Touch buttons, W and S, the arrow keys, or a gamepad.

How do you play Backyard Rally online with friends?

Open Backyard Rally on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Backyard Rally without downloading anything?

Yes. Backyard Rally runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Backyard Rally and play as a guest.

How many players can play Backyard Rally?

Backyard Rally on ChitPlay is for 2 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Backyard Rally free to play?

Yes, Backyard Rally is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Backyard Rally against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
