Baraf Pani is freeze tag in a 3D schoolyard. One player is IT. When IT tags a runner, that player freezes in place. A teammate can run over and tag a frozen player to bring them back into the chase.

Ways to play Baraf Pani

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 3 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Baraf Pani needs three or four players: one IT and the runners. Computer players fill any empty places, and IT changes from match to match.
  2. IT counts to three in the middle of the yard while the runners scatter. Then the chase is on.
  3. Move with the joystick or arrow keys. Hold Sprint for a burst of speed; after a second or so you are out of breath and have to walk a while.
  4. When IT tags a runner, that runner freezes (baraf!). A free runner who touches a frozen friend unfreezes them (pani!).
  5. IT wins if all the runners are frozen at once. The runners win if at least one is still free when time runs out.

Scoring

It is won as a side: IT by freezing every runner, the runners by keeping at least one player free until time runs out. The winners score 1.

Who wins

Play solo against computer runners, with friends on one screen, nearby or online. Bot players fill open seats.

Tips

  • As a runner, circle back to rescue frozen teammates.
  • As IT, watch for runners trying to rescue each other.
  • Use short sprints to change direction without losing control.

Questions

Can a frozen player move?

No. A teammate must tag them to unfreeze them.

Why not two players?

Freeze tag needs a friend who can unfreeze you. With only two, the first tag would end the game, so ChitPlay seats at least three and computer players fill the gaps.

Can I play with bots?

Yes. Computer players fill any open seats.

How do you play Baraf Pani online with friends?

Open Baraf Pani on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Baraf Pani without downloading anything?

Yes. Baraf Pani runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Baraf Pani and play as a guest.

How many players can play Baraf Pani?

Baraf Pani on ChitPlay is for 3 to 4 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Baraf Pani free to play?

Yes, Baraf Pani is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Baraf Pani against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
