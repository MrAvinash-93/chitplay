Bingo: play free online and learn the rules

This is Bingo the way friends play it on paper: everyone gets a 5 × 5 card with the numbers 1 to 25 in their own order. Players take turns calling a number, and it is crossed off on every card. Each full row, column or diagonal is a line and lights one letter of B-I-N-G-O. The first to finish five lines wins. Since you choose the numbers you call, there is real strategy: call the ones that complete your own lines.

Ways to play Bingo

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Each player gets a card with 1 to 25 in a different order. Only you can see your card.
  2. On your turn, type a number from 1 to 25 that has not been called yet and press ✓. You have 15 seconds, or a number is called for you.
  3. The called number is crossed off on everyone's card, yours and your friends'.
  4. Every full row, column or diagonal is a line and lights the next letter of B-I-N-G-O.

Scoring

The bar at the top shows how many letters of BINGO each player has lit. There are twelve possible lines on a card: five rows, five columns and two diagonals.

Who wins

The first to finish five lines wins. If one number finishes two cards at once, more lines wins; if still level, the player who called it wins.

Tips

  • Call numbers that sit on lines you have nearly finished.
  • The centre square is on four lines: two diagonals, a row and a column.
  • Watch your friends' letters: if someone is at BING, finish fast.

Questions

Can my friends see my card?

No. Each phone gets only its own card; the others are hidden until the game ends.

What if I type a number that was already called?

Nothing is lost: the game tells you and you can type another one before your time runs out.

How do you play Bingo online with friends?

Open Bingo on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Bingo without downloading anything?

Yes. Bingo runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Bingo and play as a guest.

How many players can play Bingo?

Bingo on ChitPlay is for 2 to 4 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Bingo free to play?

Yes, Bingo is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Bingo against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
