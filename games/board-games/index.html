Board games: play free online in 3D

The board games families have played at home for generations, set out on a mat in a 3D room: Ludo, Carrom, Chess, Snakes and Ladders, Checkers, Reversi, Mancala, Nine Men’s Morris and Four in a Row, plus the dice games Yacht and Pig.

Every game follows its usual rules, with the house rules people argue about switchable on or off. Play the computer at easy, normal or hard, invite friends to join from their own phones with a code, or pass one phone round the table.

Questions

What is the best board game to play online with friends?

Ludo is the favourite: two to four players, quick to learn and full of comebacks. Chess and Carrom are best for two.

Can I play board games online without signing up?

Yes. Every ChitPlay board game can be played as a guest, with no account and no download.
