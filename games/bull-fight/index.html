Bull Fight is a pushing contest for two, like the village games where bulls lock horns. Each player steers a bull round a round sand ring. Charge to hit hard, and push the other bull over the white line. Nobody gets hurt: it is all about strength, timing and keeping your own feet in the ring.

Ways to play Bull Fight

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Drag the stick, or use W A S D, the arrow keys or a gamepad, to move your bull.
  2. Press Charge to rush forward. If the other bull is roughly in front of you, the charge aims itself straight at it. Then wait for the bar to say CHARGE READY again.
  3. The ring and arrow under each bull show whose it is and which way it faces. Near the edge the hooves grip the sand, so you only go out if you are pushed; the rim glows red and EDGE! shows when you are close.
  4. A charging bull that hits the other one sends it flying and leaves it dizzy for a moment.
  5. Whoever is pushed over the white line loses the round. After a while the ring starts to shrink.

Scoring

Pushing the other bull out wins the round.

Who wins

The first to win three rounds takes the match.

Levels

  1. Round 1: The full ring.
  2. Every round after: The ring starts a little smaller, so a single good charge counts for more.

Tips

  • Stay near the middle: the edge is where rounds are lost.
  • Charge from the side the other bull isn't facing.
  • A missed charge leaves you wide open. Wait until you are lined up.

Questions

Is anyone hurt?

No. It is a pushing game, and the bulls just stagger for a moment.

How do two people play?

On one keyboard, one uses W A S D and Space, the other the arrows and Enter. Gamepads and online play work too.

How do you play Bull Fight online with friends?

Open Bull Fight on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Bull Fight without downloading anything?

Yes. Bull Fight runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Bull Fight and play as a guest.

How many players can play Bull Fight?

Bull Fight on ChitPlay is for 2 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Bull Fight free to play?

Yes, Bull Fight is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Bull Fight against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
