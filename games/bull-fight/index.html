Bull Fight: play free online and learn the rules

Bull Fight is a pushing contest for two, like the village games where bulls lock horns. Each player steers a bull round a round sand ring. Charge to hit hard, and push the other bull over the white line. Nobody gets hurt: it is all about strength, timing and keeping your own feet in the ring.

Ways to play Bull Fight

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 📶 Friends nearby Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Drag the stick, or use W A S D, the arrow keys or a gamepad, to move your bull.
  2. Press Charge to rush forward in the direction you are facing. Then wait for the bar under the TV to fill up again.
  3. A charging bull that hits the other one sends it flying and leaves it dizzy for a moment.
  4. Whoever is pushed over the white line loses the round. After a while the ring starts to shrink.

Scoring

Pushing the other bull out wins the round.

Who wins

The first to win three rounds takes the match.

Tips

  • Stay near the middle: the edge is where rounds are lost.
  • Charge from the side the other bull isn't facing.
  • A missed charge leaves you wide open. Wait until you are lined up.

Questions

Is anyone hurt?

No. It is a pushing game, and the bulls just stagger for a moment.

How do two people play?

On one keyboard, one uses W A S D and Space, the other the arrows and Enter. Gamepads and online play work too.
