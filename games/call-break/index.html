Call Break is a trick-taking card game for four, popular in Nepal and India. Each player calls how many tricks they expect to win, then tries to win at least that many. Spades are always trumps. Points in ChitPlay have no value: nothing is bet, bought or won.

How to play

  1. All 52 cards are dealt, 13 to each player.
  2. Each player calls a number of tricks from 1 to 8, starting with the player after the dealer.
  3. The first player leads any card. Going round, each player must play the same suit, and must beat the best card so far if they can.
  4. With none of the led suit you must play a spade, and beat the highest spade if you can. With no spades, play anything.
  5. The highest spade wins the trick; with no spade, the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.

Scoring

Make your call and you score the number you called, plus 0.1 for each extra trick. Miss it and you lose the number you called.

Who wins

After five rounds, the highest total wins.

Tips

  • Count your aces and high spades when you call, and stay a little cautious: missing a call costs a lot.
  • Lead aces early, before someone runs out of that suit and can trump it.
  • Keep track of which spades have gone.
