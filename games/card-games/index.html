Card games: play free online with friends

Shuffle up for card games that work as well at a table as they do on a phone. Call Break is the trick-taking favourite of South Asia, where spades are always trumps. Color Clash is a fast colour-and-number shedding game with +2 and Rainbow +4 cards, and Crazy Eights is the classic where an eight changes the suit.

Your hand stays hidden on your own screen, the table shows the cards everyone can see, and computer players fill any empty seat.

Each game keeps score across several rounds, so one bad hand never decides it: Call Break is played over five rounds, Color Clash to 150 points or five rounds, and Crazy Eights over three. The score for every player stays on the screen, and the rules page of each game explains the scoring with examples, so new players can join a table and keep up from the first deal.

3 card games to play

  • Color Clash 👥 2–4 players · ⏱ 10–20 minutes
    Match the colour or the number, hit them with +2 and Rainbow +4, and empty your hand first.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Call Break 👥 4 players · ⏱ 15–25 minutes
    Call your tricks, then make them. Spades are always trumps.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Crazy Eights 👥 2–4 players · ⏱ 10–15 minutes
    Match the suit or rank, and use wild eights to empty your hand first.

Questions

How do you play Call Break online?

Open Call Break on ChitPlay, bid how many tricks you will win, then win them. Spades are always trumps. Play the computer or invite three friends.

Are the card games free?

Yes, all the card games are free, with no real money, betting or prizes.
