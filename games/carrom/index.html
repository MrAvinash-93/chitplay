Carrom is the tabletop flicking game loved across South Asia. Two players take turns flicking a heavy striker from their baseline to knock coins into the four corner pockets. ChitPlay plays it on a 3D board with real physics, using simplified rules so a game fits on a phone. Tournament rules differ in some details.

How to play

  1. Seat 1 plays the white coins and seat 2 the black. There are nine of each, plus the red queen in the middle.
  2. Set your striker along the baseline with the Striker slider, choose a direction with Aim and a strength with Power, then shoot.
  3. Pocket one of your own coins and you shoot again. If you don't, the turn passes.
  4. The queen must be covered. After pocketing her, sink one of your own coins on the same shot or your next one, or she goes back to the centre.
  5. If the striker goes in a pocket it's a foul: one of your pocketed coins returns to the centre and your turn ends.
  6. The queen has to be gone before anyone's last coin can stay down.

Scoring

The winner scores 1 point for each coin the other player still has on the board, plus 3 for holding the queen.

Who wins

The first player to pocket all their coins, with the queen covered, wins. If 80 shots pass, the player with fewer coins left wins.

Tips

  • A gentle shot is often better: coins near a pocket need only a nudge.
  • Think of where the striker must touch the coin, not where the coin is.
  • Too much power sends the striker into a pocket. That foul costs you a coin.
  • Take the queen when you have an easy coin lined up to cover her.
