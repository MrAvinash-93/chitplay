Color Clash is ChitPlay's own color card game for two to four players, in the family of matching-and-shedding card games played the world over. Match the colour or the number of the card on top, use action cards to hold the others back, and be the first to empty your hand. It plays with our own deck of 108 cards.

Ways to play Color Clash

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Everyone gets seven cards. One card starts the pile.
  2. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card by colour or by number or symbol.
  3. Skip (⊘) jumps the next player. Turn Around (⇄) changes direction; with two players it works like a skip. +2 makes the next player draw two and miss their turn.
  4. A Rainbow card can go on anything and you choose the next colour. Rainbow +4 does the same, and the next player draws four and misses their turn.
  5. Nothing to play? Draw a card. You may play it if it fits; otherwise pass.

Scoring

The player who empties their hand scores the cards left in everybody else's hand: numbers count their face value, action cards 20, rainbow cards 50.

Who wins

The first to 150 points wins. If nobody gets there in five rounds, the highest score wins.

House rules you can switch on

  • Stacking: Hit with a +2? Play your own +2 or a Rainbow +4 on it and the pile-up moves on to the next player, bigger. A +4 can only be answered with another +4. Whoever can't (or won't) draws all of it and misses their turn.
  • Draw till you can play: With no card to play you keep drawing until you get one that fits, then you may play it.

Tips

  • Keep a Rainbow card for when you are stuck.
  • Save +2 and +4 for a player who is about to go out.
  • Play the colour you hold most of, so you keep your choices open.

Questions

Is Color Clash the same as other colour card games?

It belongs to the same family of matching card games, with our own deck, names and scoring.

How many can play?

Two to four. Computer players fill any empty seats, online too.

Can you stack +2 cards?

Not in the standard game: the next player draws and their turn ends. Switch on the Stacking rule before the game to pass a +2 on with your own +2 or +4.

How do you play Color Clash online with friends?

Open Color Clash on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Color Clash without downloading anything?

Yes. Color Clash runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Color Clash and play as a guest.

How many players can play Color Clash?

Color Clash on ChitPlay is for 2 to 4 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Color Clash free to play?

Yes, Color Clash is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Color Clash against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
