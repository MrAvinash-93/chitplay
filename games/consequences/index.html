Consequences is the old paper-folding story game. Each player adds one part of a story (who, met whom, where, what they said, what happened) without seeing what came before. At the end the whole story is unfolded and read out, usually to a lot of laughter.

Ways to play Consequences

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 3 to 8 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Each player in turn adds a line for the part shown, without seeing the earlier ones.
  2. With friends you type your own lines. At a table with strangers, you choose from lines we wrote, so nobody can send anything unkind.
  3. Seven parts make a story: who, met whom, where, what each said, what happened, and what the world said.
  4. The story then unfolds for everyone to read.

Scoring

There are no points: the best story wins the room.

Who wins

Everybody wins when the story makes the whole table laugh.

Tips

  • Short lines work best.
  • Keep it kind: the story is read out to everyone.

Questions

Where does the name come from?

It's a traditional parlour game played on folded paper, and the last line tells "the consequence".

Can strangers write to me?

No. At public tables everyone picks from our fixed list of lines.

How do you play Consequences online with friends?

Open Consequences on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Consequences without downloading anything?

Yes. Consequences runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Consequences and play as a guest.

How many players can play Consequences?

Consequences on ChitPlay is for 3 to 8 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Consequences free to play?

Yes, Consequences is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Consequences against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
