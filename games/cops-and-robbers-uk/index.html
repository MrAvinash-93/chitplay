How to play King, Queen, Bobby & Robber

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Queen, Bobby, Robber.
  2. The Bobby reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Bobby studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Robber.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: Everyone gives an alibi, then the Bobby makes an arrest. A wrong arrest costs the Bobby 200 and the Robber gets away with 1000.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Tips to win King, Queen, Bobby & Robber

  • As the Robber: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Bobby: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Robber.
  • Listen to the alibis. The vaguest one is often the Robber.
