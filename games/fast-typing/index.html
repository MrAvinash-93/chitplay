Fast Typing is a short typing race. A word appears and you type it as fast as you can, then press ✓. The words start short and get longer: four letters at first, up to ten by the end. Speed scores, but accuracy decides: one wrong letter and the word is worth nothing. At the end you see your speed in letters per second. Play alone to beat your best, or race up to three friends on their own phones.

Ways to play Fast Typing

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. "Get ready" shows for a second, then the word appears.
  2. Type the word on the letters keypad (or your keyboard) and press ✓. You get one try per word.
  3. The bar under the word shows the time left: four seconds plus one for each letter.
  4. Each phone times its own player from the moment the word appeared there, so a slow connection never costs you speed.

Scoring

A right word scores ten points for every letter per second: six letters in two seconds is 30. At least 5 for any right word, at most 60. A wrong letter scores nothing for that word. Medals at 120, 200, 280 and 360 points.

Who wins

After ten words, the player with the most points wins.

Tips

  • Read the whole word before you start typing.
  • Check the word above the keys before pressing ✓: a slip costs the whole word.
  • On a computer, type on your keyboard and press Enter.

Questions

Is this a typing speed test?

Yes. At the end it shows your average speed in letters per second over the words you got right, so you can track how you improve.

Which language are the words in?

Everyday English words, like animals, fruits and things at home, from four to ten letters long.

How do you play Fast Typing online with friends?

Open Fast Typing on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Fast Typing without downloading anything?

Yes. Fast Typing runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Fast Typing and play as a guest.

How many players can play Fast Typing?

Fast Typing on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Fast Typing free to play?

Yes, Fast Typing is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Fast Typing against the computer?

Yes. You can play Fast Typing on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
