Flying Chit is ChitPlay's own endless 3D game. A folded paper chit has grown two little wings and is gliding through a classroom that never ends. You steer it in every direction with the stick. It slowly runs out of lift, and only flying through the golden bangles keeps it in the air. Ceiling fans and stacks of books knock the paper out of it. The room gets faster and busier the further you go.

Ways to play Flying Chit

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 📶 Friends nearby Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Drag the stick, or use the arrow keys, W A S D or a gamepad, to steer the chit up, down, left and right.
  2. Fly through a golden bangle to win back some lift. The bar under your score shows how much is left.
  3. Bangles one after another build a combo: ×2, ×3, up to ×5 points each.
  4. Missing a bangle ends the combo. Hitting a fan or a pile of books costs a lot of lift.
  5. When the lift runs out the chit glides to the floor and your flight is over.

Scoring

Each bangle scores its combo: 1, then 2, 3, 4 and 5 points while the chain lasts. Medals at 40, 120, 250 and 500 points.

Who wins

Alone, you are chasing your own best score. With others, everyone flies at once and the highest score wins.

Tips

  • Line up the next bangle early: small, steady movements beat big swerves.
  • A combo is worth more than a single safe bangle, but not more than your lift.
  • Watch for a fan just behind a bangle and plan your way out before you go in.

Questions

Does Flying Chit ever end?

No. The bangles get smaller and the room faster, so there is always a better score to chase.

Can two people play on one screen?

Yes. Choose Single Phone: one player uses W A S D and the other the arrow keys, or each uses a gamepad.

Is it like other flying games?

It is our own idea: you steer freely in 3D and keep the chit up by flying through bangles, instead of tapping to flap.
