Flying Chit is ChitPlay's own endless 3D game. A folded paper chit has grown two little wings and is gliding through a classroom that never ends. You steer it in every direction with the stick. It slowly runs out of lift, and only flying through the golden bangles keeps it in the air. Ceiling fans and stacks of books knock the paper out of it. The room gets faster and busier the further you go. Earn coins through play and spend them on new paper-chit looks; every style is cosmetic and keeps the same flight physics.

Ways to play Flying Chit

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Drag the stick, or use the arrow keys, W A S D or a gamepad, to steer the chit up, down, left and right.
  2. A ring shows where your chit will cross the next bangle: it turns green when you are on target. Near a bangle the chit is drawn gently towards its middle.
  3. Fly through a golden bangle to win back some lift. The bar under your score shows how much is left.
  4. Bangles one after another build a combo: ×2, ×3, up to ×5 points each.
  5. Missing a bangle ends the combo. Hitting a fan or a pile of books costs a lot of lift.
  6. When the lift runs out the chit glides to the floor and your flight is over.
  7. Every new level starts with a big golden bangle: 10 points and full lift.

Scoring

Each bangle scores its combo: 1, then 2, 3, 4 and 5 points while the chain lasts. Medals at 50, 200, 500 and 1000 points.

Who wins

Alone, you are chasing your own best score. With others, everyone flies at once and the highest score wins.

Levels

  1. Morning class: Only stacks of books get in the way.
  2. Fans on: Ceiling fans join the books.
  3. Swinging bangles: Some bangles swing from side to side. Aim where the bangle will be.
  4. Paper storm: The evening light comes in, paper flies through the air, and some books drift across too.
  5. Night class (level 5 and up): The tube lights are on, and the fans bob up and down as well.
  6. Golden bangle: Every new level opens with a big golden bangle in the middle: 10 points and full lift.

Tips

  • Watch the aim ring, not the chit: when it is green, hold still.
  • Line up the next bangle early: small, steady movements beat big swerves.
  • A combo is worth more than a single safe bangle, but not more than your lift.
  • Watch for a fan just behind a bangle and plan your way out before you go in.

Questions

Does Flying Chit ever end?

No. The bangles get smaller and the room faster, so there is always a better score to chase.

Can I unlock different paper chits?

Yes. Earn coins through play, then open Game Styles to unlock a Blueprint Glider, Golden Origami, Neon Comet or Rose Kite. These change the 3D look, not the flight physics.

Can two people play on one screen?

Yes. Choose Single Phone: one player uses W A S D and the other the arrow keys, or each uses a gamepad.

Is it like other flying games?

It is our own idea: you steer freely in 3D and keep the chit up by flying through bangles, instead of tapping to flap.

How do you play Flying Chit online with friends?

Open Flying Chit on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Flying Chit without downloading anything?

Yes. Flying Chit runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Flying Chit and play as a guest.

How many players can play Flying Chit?

Flying Chit on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Flying Chit free to play?

Yes, Flying Chit is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Flying Chit against the computer?

Yes. You can play Flying Chit on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
