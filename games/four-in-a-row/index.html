Four in a Row is a simple game of lines and gravity for two. Discs drop to the lowest free space in a column, so every move also changes what's possible above it. You can learn it in seconds, yet good players plan several moves ahead.

How to play

  1. Players take turns dropping one disc into any column with room left.
  2. The disc falls to the lowest free space in that column.
  3. Make a line of four of your discs, across, up or diagonally, and you win at once.

Scoring

A game ends in a win or a draw. The result table shows 1 for the winner.

Who wins

The first line of four wins. If the grid fills up with no line, it's a draw.

Tips

  • Start in the middle column: more lines of four pass through it.
  • Make two threats at once: the other player can only block one.
  • Before dropping a disc, check what it lets the other player do on top of it.

Questions

Who goes first?

The first player at the table. Going first is an advantage, so take turns over several games.

Is the grid the standard size?

Yes: seven columns and six rows.
