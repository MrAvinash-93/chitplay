How to play Gendarmes et Voleurs

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Queen, Gendarme, Thief.
  2. The Gendarme reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Gendarme studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Thief.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: The Gendarme looks for the Thief among the other three. In the last round every point counts double! If the Thief escapes, they win 1000.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Can you cheat in Gendarmes et Voleurs?

In CHITPLAY the Gendarme can press 🤫 Cheat to try to peek at a chit. Seven times in ten nobody notices, and you see one innocent player's chit, never the Thief's. Three times in ten you are caught: you see nothing, you lose 100 points, and the table lets you hear about it. Computer players try it too.

Tips to win Gendarmes et Voleurs

  • As the Thief: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Gendarme: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Thief.
  • The last round counts double. A comeback is always possible.
