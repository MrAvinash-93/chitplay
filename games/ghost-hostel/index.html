Ghost Hostel is a game of building up and holding on. It is night in an old hostel and each player has a room with a bed and a door. A ghost lives in the corridor. It sleeps for a few seconds, then picks the weakest door and starts banging on it. Every coin you earn goes into a better bed, a stronger door, fans that blow the ghost back, or a quick repair.

Ways to play Ghost Hostel

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Your bed earns coins every second. A better bed earns more.
  2. Use the arrows or the pad to choose Bed, Door, Fan or Fix, and press Buy.
  3. Door: more strength, and it is fixed up to its new strength. Fix: mends part of the door, then needs a few seconds before the next fix.
  4. Fans blow at the ghost while it is at your door. Hurt it enough and it floats back to its corner to recover.
  5. The ghost gets stronger every thirty seconds, and angrier in the last forty. If your door breaks, you are caught and out.

Scoring

You score every coin your bed earned, plus 100 for lasting till sunrise and 150 more if the ghost is blown away for good.

Who wins

Everyone still in their room at sunrise wins. Blow the ghost away completely and the night ends early. If everyone is caught, the ghost wins.

Tips

  • A better bed early pays for everything later.
  • Buy your first fan before the ghost reaches your door.
  • Upgrade the door before it is nearly broken: an upgrade adds its full extra strength.
