Hoop Flick: play free online and learn the rules

Hoop Flick is a one-finger basketball game. The ball sits in a hoop on one side of the court and the next hoop waits on the other side. Pull back, aim and let go: the dotted line shows where the ball will fly. Drop it through the hoop and a new one appears on the other side. Higher, lower, and after a while the hoops start to move.

  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.

  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Drag the stick backwards, like a slingshot: the ball will fly the opposite way. The further you pull, the harder the throw.
  2. Let go of the stick to throw. With keys, use the left and right arrows to turn the aim, up and down for power, and Space to throw.
  3. Get the ball to fall through the next hoop from above.
  4. Each hoop has twelve seconds: the red bar shows when time is running out.
  5. Miss and the ball drops out of the court: the run is over.

Scoring

A basket that touches the rim scores 1. A clean swish scores 2, and swishes in a row score more: up to 5 points each. Medals at 20, 50, 100 and 200 points.

Who wins

Alone, beat your own best. With a friend, each of you plays your own half of the court and the higher score wins.

Tips

  • Watch the dotted line, not the ball: it shows the whole path.
  • A higher, softer arc drops in more cleanly than a flat, hard throw.
  • On moving hoops, throw when the hoop is coming towards the middle.

Questions

Does it end?

No. The hoops keep coming until you miss.

Can two people play?

Yes, on one screen, nearby or online. Each player has their own half of the court.
