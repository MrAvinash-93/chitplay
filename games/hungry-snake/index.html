Hungry Snake: play free online and learn the rules

A long-time classic with an appetite: your snake loves snacks. Every one makes it longer and faster, so the screen slowly fills up with snake. With other players around, the same small field gets crowded very quickly.

Ways to play Hungry Snake

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Steer your snake up, down, left or right. It never stops moving.
  2. Eat a snack to score a point and grow longer.
  3. Hitting a wall, yourself or another snake ends your game.
  4. With others, the last snake moving gets 3 bonus points.

Scoring

One point per snack, plus 3 for being the last one left.

Who wins

Alone, beat your own best score. With others, the highest score wins.

Levels

  1. Open field: Nothing on the field but the samosa.
  2. First bricks: Every six samosas the snakes speed up and a short brick wall goes up somewhere on the field, never close to anyone's head.
  3. Brick maze (level 3 and up): The walls add up, so the field gets tighter as the snakes get longer.
  4. Golden samosa: Every new level a golden samosa appears for seven seconds: 3 points to whoever gets there first. It blinks just before it goes.

Tips

  • Move along the edges when you get long: it keeps the middle open.
  • Plan your way out before you go in for a snack.

Questions

Does it get harder?

Yes. Every six samosas is a new level: the snakes speed up and a brick wall goes up on the field.

How many can play?

Up to four, with computer players filling any seats.

How do you play Hungry Snake online with friends?

Open Hungry Snake on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Hungry Snake without downloading anything?

Yes. Hungry Snake runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Hungry Snake and play as a guest.

How many players can play Hungry Snake?

Hungry Snake on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Hungry Snake free to play?

Yes, Hungry Snake is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Hungry Snake against the computer?

Yes. You can play Hungry Snake on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
