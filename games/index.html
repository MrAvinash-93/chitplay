Questions

Are Raja Rani Chor Police, Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi and King Queen Thief Police the same game?

Yes. They are names for the same four-player paper chit game. The role names, the points and one small rule change from place to place.

Which chit game should I play?

Pick the name you grew up with. ChitPlay opens the game of your own country first, and you can swipe to any other.

Can I play a chit game online alone?

Yes. In CHITPLAY three computer players sit with you. You can also invite up to three friends on their own phones, or just watch.
