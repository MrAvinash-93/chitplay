Arcade games (3D, on the TV)

  • Flying Chit: Steer a winged chit through bangles in an endless classroom. Chain them for combos. 1–4 players, 1–5 minutes.
  • Tunnel Race: A paper plane in an endless tunnel. Steer round the walls and grab the gems. 1–4 players, 1–5 minutes.
  • Hoop Flick: Pull back, let go, and drop the ball through the next hoop. Swish them in a row for combos. 1–2 players, 1–5 minutes.
  • Ghost Hostel: One night in a haunted hostel. Sleep for coins, build up your door and fans, and last till sunrise. 1–4 players, 3 minutes.
  • Knife Throw: Throw knives into a spinning target without touching the others. Beat level after level. 1–4 players, 1–5 minutes.
  • Ship Fight: Two ships, cannons firing non-stop. Dodge, line up your shots and sink your friend. 2 players, 2–5 minutes.
  • Bull Fight: Two bulls, one sand ring. Charge, lock horns and push the other one out. 2 players, 2–5 minutes.
  • Road Dash: An auto-rickshaw on a road that never ends. Dodge cows and buses, grab the coins. 1–4 players, 1–5 minutes.
  • Backyard Rally: Bat and ball in the backyard. The rally keeps getting faster. First to seven. 2 players, 2–4 minutes.
  • Hungry Snake: Eat the snacks, grow longer, and don’t bite anything. Faster with every bite. 1–4 players, 1–5 minutes.

Board games

  • Ludo: Race your four tokens home. Roll a six to start, and send rivals back. 2–4 players, 20–40 minutes.
  • Carrom: Flick the striker, pocket your coins, and cover the queen. 2 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Chess: The full game: castling, en passant, promotion and checkmate. 2 players, 10–40 minutes.
  • Snakes and Ladders: Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes, land exactly on 100. 2–4 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Checkers: Jump the other side’s pieces and crown your kings. 2 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Four in a Row: Drop discs and line up four before the other player does. 2 players, 5–10 minutes.
  • Reversi: Trap the other colour between yours and flip it to your side. 2 players, 10–15 minutes.
  • Mancala: Sow your seeds round the board, and capture to fill your store. 2 players, 5–15 minutes.
  • Nine Men’s Morris: Make lines of three to take pieces in this very old strategy game. 2 players, 10–20 minutes.

Card games

  • Color Clash: Match the colour or the number, hit them with +2 and Rainbow +4, and empty your hand first. 2–4 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Call Break: Call your tricks, then make them. Spades are always trumps. 4 players, 15–25 minutes.
  • Crazy Eights: Match the suit or rank, and use wild eights to empty your hand first. 2–4 players, 10–15 minutes.

Puzzle and pen-and-paper games

  • Tic-Tac-Toe: Three in a line. A minute to learn, and always ready for a rematch. 2 players, 1–3 minutes.
  • Dots and Boxes: Draw lines, close boxes, and take another turn each time you do. 2–4 players, 5–10 minutes.
  • Memory Match: Turn two tiles and remember what you saw. Pairs go to the sharpest. 2–4 players, 5–10 minutes.

Dice games

  • Yacht: Five dice, three rolls, twelve categories. Pick your scores wisely. 2–4 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Pig: Keep rolling or bank your points, but a 1 loses the whole turn. 2–4 players, 5–10 minutes.

Party games

  • Who Is the Spy?: Everyone shares a secret word except the spy. Find them from the clues. 4–10 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Werewolf Night: Secret roles, quiet nights and loud mornings. Find the werewolves before they take the village. 5–10 players, 10–20 minutes.
  • Truth or Dare: Friendly truths and fun dares for every age. Skip any prompt. 2–10 players, 10–30 minutes.
  • Riddle Chits: Open a riddle and pick the answer. A hint costs you a point. 2–10 players, 5–15 minutes.
  • Majority Vote: Guess what most of the table will choose. Think like the crowd. 3–10 players, 5–10 minutes.
  • Consequences: Write a story one folded line at a time, then unfold the whole tale. 3–8 players, 5–10 minutes.

Questions

Are Raja Rani Chor Police, Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi and King Queen Thief Police the same game?

Yes. They are names for the same four-player paper chit game. The role names, the points and one small rule change from place to place.

Which chit game should I play?

Pick the name you grew up with. ChitPlay opens the game of your own country first, and you can swipe to any other.

Can I play a chit game online alone?

Yes. In CHITPLAY three computer players sit with you. You can also invite up to three friends on their own phones, or just watch.
