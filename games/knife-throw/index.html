Knife Throw: play free online and learn the rules

Knife Throw is a game of timing. A round wooden target spins in front of you, and every knife you throw has to land in a free spot between the knives already in it. Throw all the knives of a level and the target breaks: the next one spins faster, changes direction or already has knives in the way. Every fifth level is a boss target.

How to play

  1. Tap the screen, press the Throw button, Space or Enter, or a gamepad button to throw a knife. It always flies straight up into the bottom of the target.
  2. Wait for a gap: if your knife touches one already in the target, the run is over.
  3. Throw all the knives shown at the bottom of the screen to finish the level.
  4. Hit an apple on the rim for 5 bonus points.
  5. Every fifth level is a boss: it changes speed and direction.
  6. Each level has a time limit (the thin bar at the bottom): throw all its knives before it runs out.

Scoring

Each knife that sticks scores 1, each apple 5, and finishing a level scores twice the level number. Medals at 25, 60, 120 and 250 points.

Who wins

Alone, beat your own best. With others, everyone throws at their own target at the same time and the highest score wins.

Tips

  • Throw just after a knife passes the bottom, not just before the next one arrives.
  • On targets that change direction, wait for the change before you throw.
  • Apples are worth a risk early in a level, not when the target is full.

Questions

Does it ever end?

No: the levels keep coming, and you play until a knife touches another.

Can friends play together?

Yes, up to four, each with their own target, on one screen, nearby or online.
