Lapa Chupi: play free online and learn the rules

Lapa Chupi is a schoolyard hide-and-seek game. One seeker searches the yard while the others find cover and try to stay hidden. Hiders who move near the seeker are spotted; the seeker wins by finding everyone before time runs out.

Ways to play Lapa Chupi

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. A different player is the seeker each match. The seeker waits by the wall with eyes shut while everyone else gets five seconds to hide.
  2. Use the joystick or arrow keys to move around the 3D playground. Hold Sprint to run faster, but only for a second or so before you are out of breath.
  3. Stop by a bush, bench or playground prop and keep still to hide. Moving, or letting the seeker come close, gives you away.
  4. The seeker wins by finding every hider. Any hiders still hidden when the timer ends win together.
  5. Hidden players’ locations are concealed from other players in online rooms.

Scoring

The seeker scores one for each hider found and wins by finding them all. Hiders still hidden when time runs out win together and score more than any number of finds.

Who wins

Play with computer players, friends on one screen, nearby or online. Bot players fill open seats.

Tips

  • As a hider, stop near cover before the seeker comes close.
  • As the seeker, check the corners and cover objects first.
  • Save Sprint for a quick escape rather than running in circles.

Questions

Can I play solo?

Yes. Computer players fill the other seats.

Can online players see where I hide?

No. Hidden player coordinates are removed from each other player’s game updates.

How do you play Lapa Chupi online with friends?

Open Lapa Chupi on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Lapa Chupi without downloading anything?

Yes. Lapa Chupi runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Lapa Chupi and play as a guest.

How many players can play Lapa Chupi?

Lapa Chupi on ChitPlay is for 2 to 4 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Lapa Chupi free to play?

Yes, Lapa Chupi is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Lapa Chupi against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
