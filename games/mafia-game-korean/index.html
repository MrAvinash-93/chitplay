How to play Mafia Game

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: Citizen, Doctor, Police, Mafia.
  2. The Police reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Police studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Mafia.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: At night the Mafia attacks someone and the Doctor tries to save one player. An unsaved victim scores 0 this round. By day, the Police accuses. If the Mafia escapes, they take 1000.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

The classic game needs exactly four players. In CHITPLAY you can play with three computer players, invite up to three friends on their own phones, pass a single phone around, or just watch.

Is Mafia Game only for children?

No. Children, teenagers, parents and grandparents all play it. It takes a minute to learn, and bluffing is just as much fun at any age.

Is Mafia Game a good indoor game?

Yes. On paper all you need is a page and a pen, so it works at home, in a classroom, on a train or during a power cut. Online you only need a phone.

Can you cheat in Mafia Game?

In CHITPLAY the Police can press 🤫 Cheat to try to peek at a chit. Seven times in ten nobody notices, and you see one innocent player's chit, never the Mafia's. Three times in ten you are caught: you see nothing, you lose 100 points, and the table lets you hear about it. Computer players try it too.

Tips to win Mafia Game

  • As the Mafia: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Police: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Mafia.
  • Watch who looks relieved after the night. The Mafia already knows who was attacked.
