How to play Mafia: Commissar

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: Civilian, Doctor, Commissar, Mafia.
  2. The Commissar reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Commissar studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Mafia.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: At night the Commissar secretly checks one player: sometimes that catches the Mafia straight away, sometimes it only clears someone. By day, the Commissar accuses. If the Mafia escapes, they take 1000.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Can you cheat in Mafia: Commissar?

In CHITPLAY the Commissar can press 🤫 Cheat to try to peek at a chit. Seven times in ten nobody notices, and you see one innocent player's chit, never the Mafia's. Three times in ten you are caught: you see nothing, you lose 100 points, and the table lets you hear about it. Computer players try it too.

Tips to win Mafia: Commissar

  • As the Mafia: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Commissar: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Mafia.
  • Your night check either catches the Mafia straight away or clears someone. Either way, one fewer suspect.
