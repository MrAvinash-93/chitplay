Mancala is a family of counting games that are among the oldest still played, known across Africa and Asia under many names. ChitPlay plays a common modern version with six pits a side and four seeds in each.

How to play

  1. Each player has six pits on the near side and a store at the right-hand end. Tap one of your pits that holds seeds.
  2. All the seeds in that pit are sown one by one, anticlockwise, into the next pits. You pass your own store, dropping one in, but skip the other player's.
  3. If your last seed lands in your own store, you go again.
  4. If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, and the pit opposite has seeds, you capture them all, plus your own seed.
  5. When one side has no seeds left, the other player keeps the seeds remaining on their side.

Scoring

Each seed in your store is worth one point.

Who wins

The player with the most seeds in their store wins. Equal counts are a draw.

Tips

  • Look for a pit whose seeds end exactly in your store, then use the extra turn to set up another.
  • Empty pits on your side are capture chances. Watch the seeds opposite them.
  • A big pile on your side is a target: move it before it's captured.

Questions

How many seeds are there?

Forty-eight: four in each of the twelve pits.

Is this the only way to play Mancala?

No, there are hundreds of traditional versions. This is one of the most widely played.
