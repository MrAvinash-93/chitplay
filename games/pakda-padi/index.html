Pakda Padi is a quick game of tag in a 3D playground. One player starts as IT. Catch someone and the tag passes to them. Keep moving: the player who spends the least time as IT wins when the timer runs out.

Ways to play Pakda Padi

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Move around the playground with the joystick or arrow keys. Hold Sprint for a burst of speed; after a second or so you are out of breath and have to walk a while.
  2. The player marked IT counts to three, then chases the others. Get close enough to pass the tag.
  3. Once tagged, turn around and chase someone else. No tag-backs: for two seconds you can’t tag the player who just tagged you.
  4. When time runs out, the player who spent the least time as IT wins. A tie is possible.

Scoring

The player with the least time spent as IT wins. Tags are counted on the scoreboard.

Who wins

Play with computer players, friends on one screen, nearby or online. Bot players fill open seats.

Tips

  • Change direction just before IT reaches you.
  • As IT, cut off the path instead of following directly behind.
  • Sprint in short bursts: run out of breath and you are easy to catch.
