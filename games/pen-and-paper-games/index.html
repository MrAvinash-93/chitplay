Pen and paper games: the school classics online

The games from the back of a notebook: Tic-Tac-Toe, Dots and Boxes, Bingo with a 5 × 5 card of 1 to 25, and Memory Match, quick to learn and always ready for a rematch.

They are drawn on paper on the mat in the 3D room, and you can play them against the computer, with a friend on one phone, or with friends on their own phones.

They are small games with real thinking in them. Tic-Tac-Toe teaches you to look one move ahead and block a line before it is made. Dots and Boxes rewards patience: the player who avoids giving away the last free box usually wins the chain. Memory Match is all about concentration, remembering where each picture was when somebody else turned it over. A round takes a minute or two, so they are perfect for a short break or for playing with younger brothers, sisters and grandparents.

4 pen and paper games to play

  • Tic-Tac-Toe 👥 2 players · ⏱ 1–3 minutes
    Three in a line. A minute to learn, and always ready for a rematch.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Dots and Boxes 👥 2–4 players · ⏱ 5–10 minutes
    Draw lines, close boxes, and take another turn each time you do.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Bingo 👥 2–4 players · ⏱ 3–5 minutes
    Your own 5 × 5 card of 1 to 25. Take turns calling numbers; the first to finish five lines shouts BINGO.
    ▶ Play Rules and tips
  • Memory Match 👥 2–4 players · ⏱ 5–10 minutes
    Turn two tiles and remember what you saw. Pairs go to the sharpest.

Questions

Can you win every game of Tic-Tac-Toe?

No. With perfect play from both sides it is always a draw, so the fun is in catching a mistake.

How do you win Dots and Boxes?

Close more boxes than anyone else. Closing a box gives you another turn, so avoid drawing the third side of a box.
