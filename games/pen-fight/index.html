Pen Fight: play free online and learn the rules

Pen Fight brings the school-desk flicking game to a 3D tabletop. Both players have one flick in the three-second window. Aim toward the other pen: a faster swipe sends yours harder, and collisions can knock a pen off the desk. The first player to win three rounds takes the match.

Ways to play Pen Fight

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Swipe toward your rival within three seconds. The Flick button is an accessible alternative.
  2. A faster swipe sends your pen harder; your direction controls its path.
  3. Pens slide and collide automatically. A pen knocked over the edge or toppled by an impact loses the round.
  4. If both pens stay on the desk, the one with the stronger final position wins; very close rounds are draws.
  5. First to three round wins takes the match. Three rounds where both players miss end in a tie.

Scoring

Win a round by knocking the rival pen off the desk, toppling it with a strong collision or outlasting it. A match is first to three round wins.

Who wins

Play solo against a bot, with a friend on one screen, nearby or online. Unlock pen and desk looks with earned coins; styles are cosmetic only.

Tips

  • Swipe toward the other pen to meet it head-on.
  • A harder flick can knock a rival away, but a wild angle can send your own pen off the desk.
  • If you miss the three-second window, you can retry in the next round.

Questions

Can I play alone?

Yes. Start a two-player game and the computer fills the other seat.

Can I retry a missed flick?

Yes. A missed round is shown clearly, then the next round starts automatically.

Do pen styles change the game?

No. All unlockable pen and desk styles are cosmetic only.

How do you play Pen Fight online with friends?

Open Pen Fight on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Pen Fight without downloading anything?

Yes. Pen Fight runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Pen Fight and play as a guest.

How many players can play Pen Fight?

Pen Fight on ChitPlay is for 2 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Pen Fight free to play?

Yes, Pen Fight is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Pen Fight against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
