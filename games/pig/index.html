Pig: play free online and learn the rules

▶ Play Pig now

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • ⏱ 5–10 minutes
  • 👤 guest play
  • 🌍 online opponents
  • 🤖 computer players
  • 📶 friends nearby
  • 🆓 free, no sign-up

Pig is a traditional dice game for any number of players, and it needs only one die. All that matters is your nerve: every roll adds to your points for the turn, but a single 1 wipes them out. The points have no value: nothing is bet, bought or won.

Ways to play Pig

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 📶 Friends nearby Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. On your turn, roll the die. Any number from 2 to 6 is added to your total for this turn.
  2. Roll again as often as you like, or hold to bank this turn's total.
  3. If you roll a 1, you lose this turn's total and the turn passes on.

Scoring

Only banked points count towards your score.

Who wins

The first player to bank 50 points wins.

Tips

  • Many players hold at around 20 on a turn. Every roll has a one-in-six chance of a 1.
  • Behind near the end? Take more risks. Ahead? Bank sooner.

Questions

Why is it called Pig?

Nobody knows for sure. The game has been played under that name since at least the 1940s.

How long does a game take?

About five to ten minutes.
