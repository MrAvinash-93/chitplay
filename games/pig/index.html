Pig: play free online and learn the rules

Pig is a traditional dice game for any number of players, and it needs only one die. All that matters is your nerve: every roll adds to your points for the turn, but a single 1 wipes them out. The points have no value: nothing is bet, bought or won.

Ways to play Pig

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. On your turn, roll the die. Any number from 2 to 6 is added to your total for this turn.
  2. Roll again as often as you like, or hold to bank this turn's total.
  3. If you roll a 1, you lose this turn's total and the turn passes on.

Scoring

Only banked points count towards your score.

Who wins

Reach the target (50 in Quick, 100 in Classic and Two dice) and everyone else still gets one last turn to beat you. The highest bank after that wins.

Levels

  1. Quick: One die, first to 50.
  2. Classic: One die, first to 100: the traditional game.
  3. Two dice: Roll a pair, first to 100. A single 1 loses the turn total, two 1s lose everything you have banked, and doubles must be rolled again.

Tips

  • Many players hold at around 20 on a turn. Every roll has a one-in-six chance of a 1.
  • Behind near the end? Take more risks. Ahead? Bank sooner.

Questions

Why is it called Pig?

Nobody knows for sure. The game has been played under that name since at least the 1940s.

How long does a game take?

About five to ten minutes.

How do you play Pig online with friends?

Open Pig on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Pig without downloading anything?

Yes. Pig runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Pig and play as a guest.

How many players can play Pig?

Pig on ChitPlay is for 2 to 4 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Pig free to play?

Yes, Pig is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Pig against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
