How to play Policías y Ladrones

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Queen, Police, Robber.
  2. The Police reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Police studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Robber.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: Somebody can’t stop giggling. It’s usually the Robber, but not always! The Police decides who to arrest. If the Robber escapes, they win 1000.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Cómo jugar Policías y Ladrones con papelitos

Cuatro papelitos doblados: Rey, Reina, Policía y Ladrón. Cada quien saca uno. El Policía se descubre y tiene que adivinar quién es el Ladrón. Si acierta, gana 500 puntos; si falla, el Ladrón se lleva 1000. Ojo: alguien siempre se ríe, y casi siempre es el Ladrón.

Can you cheat in Policías y Ladrones?

In CHITPLAY the Police can press 🤫 Cheat to try to peek at a chit. Seven times in ten nobody notices, and you see one innocent player's chit, never the Robber's. Three times in ten you are caught: you see nothing, you lose 100 points, and the table lets you hear about it. Computer players try it too.

Tips to win Policías y Ladrones

  • As the Robber: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Police: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Robber.
  • Someone always giggles. It's usually the Robber, but a smart Robber might make someone else laugh.
