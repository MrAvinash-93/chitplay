Puzzle and brain games: free online, no download

Short games that make you think fast: slide the cars out of a parking lot in Unblock Puzzle, guess a hidden word one letter at a time in Word Guess, remember longer and longer numbers in Number Memory, name the colour and not the word in the Stroop Test, type words against the clock in Fast Typing, and test your reflexes in Reaction Time.

Each one takes a minute or two and keeps your best score on your device, so they are perfect for a quick break. Play alone to beat your record, or race up to three friends on their own phones: every phone times its own player, so a slow connection never costs anyone points.

They train real skills too. Unblock Puzzle is pure planning: every lot has been solved by computer, and the fewest moves are shown, so you know how close you came. Number Memory measures how many digits you can hold at once, Fast Typing shows your speed in letters per second, and Word Guess and Fast Typing practise everyday English words.
