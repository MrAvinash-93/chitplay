Racing and tunnel games: free online in 3D

Fast 3D games where you steer through something that never ends. In Turbo Tunnel you drive a car round the inside of a tunnel, dodging traffic, lasers and falling rocks; Tunnel Race sends a paper plane down a turning tunnel; Road Dash puts you in an auto-rickshaw on a busy road; and Flying Chit steers a winged chit through classroom bangles.

Every run gets faster and brings new obstacles, but nothing appears too late to dodge: each obstacle is placed so there is always a way through. Play alone for a high score, or race up to three friends on one screen or on their own phones, with no download and no sign-up.

On a phone you steer with the controls on screen; on a computer use the arrow keys or W A S D, or plug in a gamepad. The graphics adjust themselves to keep the game smooth on older phones.
