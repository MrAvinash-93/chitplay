How to play Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Minister, Soldier, Thief.
  2. The King reveals first and calls for the Minister.
  3. The Minister studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Thief.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: The King reveals himself and asks “Who is my Minister?”. The Minister must find the Thief between the last two. Guess wrong and the Minister and the Thief swap points.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.
