How to play Raja Rani Chor Police

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Queen, Police, Thief.
  2. The Police reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Police studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Thief.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: The Police must find the Thief among the other three. Guess wrong and the Police and the Thief swap points.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Tips to win Raja Rani Chor Police

  • As the Thief: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Police: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Thief.
  • A wrong guess swaps your points with the Thief's, so think before you point.
