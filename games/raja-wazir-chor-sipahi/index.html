How to play Raja Wazir Chor Sipahi

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Minister, Soldier, Thief.
  2. The Minister reveals their chit. Nobody else says who they are: everyone keeps a straight face.
  3. The Minister studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Thief.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: Only the Minister steps forward; nobody else says who they are. The Minister must find the Thief among the other three. Guess wrong and the Minister and the Thief swap points.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Tips to win Raja Wazir Chor Sipahi

  • As the Thief: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Minister: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Thief.
  • A wrong guess swaps your points with the Thief's, so think before you point.
