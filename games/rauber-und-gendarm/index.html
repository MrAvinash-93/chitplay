How to play Räuber und Gendarm

  1. Four players each draw a folded paper chit. Each chit hides one role: King, Queen, Gendarme, Robber.
  2. The Gendarme reveals their chit. Everyone else keeps a straight face.
  3. The Gendarme studies the faces and accuses one player of being the Robber.
  4. Everyone opens their chit and scores: A witness gives the Gendarme a tip, but witnesses are sometimes wrong! Then comes the arrest. If the Robber escapes, they get 1000.
  5. Play 3, 5, 7, 10 or 20 rounds. The highest total wins.

Tips to win Räuber und Gendarm

  • As the Robber: act exactly like everyone else. Don't over-explain and don't go too quiet.
  • As the Gendarme: watch who looks away, laughs too much, or rushes to say “it's not me”.
  • As a royal: acting a little nervous can pull suspicion away from the real Robber.
  • Witnesses are right about two times in three, so trust the tip but check the faces too.
