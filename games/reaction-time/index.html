Reaction Time is a quick test of your reflexes that you can turn into a race with friends. The screen is red; at a random moment it turns green, and you tap as fast as you can. Five rounds, with blue fake signals in the middle rounds to catch anyone who guesses. Your time is measured in milliseconds on your own phone, so it is fair even when you play friends far away.

Ways to play Reaction Time

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Wait while the screen is red. When it turns green, tap anywhere (or press Space, Enter or a gamepad button) as fast as you can.
  2. Tapping before green, or on the blue fake signal in rounds 3 and 4, is a false start and scores nothing for that round.
  3. Your time is measured on your own phone, from the moment green appeared on your screen until your tap, so a slow connection never makes you slower. A time under 100 ms counts as a guess.
  4. There are five rounds. After each one you see everybody's time, and the fastest at the table gets 50 bonus points.

Scoring

Each round scores 500 minus your time in milliseconds: a 250 ms reaction scores 250. False starts and missed rounds score 0. Medals at 600, 900, 1100 and 1300 points.

Who wins

The most points after five rounds wins. Alone, beat your own best and earn the medals.

Tips

  • Look at the middle of the screen, not at your finger.
  • Keep your finger resting just above the screen.
  • In rounds 3 and 4, green is the only colour that counts.

Questions

What is a good reaction time?

Most people react to something they see in about 250 to 300 milliseconds. Under 200 ms is very fast.

Is it fair when playing online?

Yes. Every phone times its own player from the moment green appears on that screen, so network delay never changes anyone's time.

How do you play Reaction Time online with friends?

Open Reaction Time on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Reaction Time without downloading anything?

Yes. Reaction Time runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Reaction Time and play as a guest.

How many players can play Reaction Time?

Reaction Time on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Reaction Time free to play?

Yes, Reaction Time is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Reaction Time against the computer?

Yes. You can play Reaction Time on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
