Reversi is a game of sudden reversals, invented in Victorian England. Two players place discs on an 8×8 board, and any discs trapped in a line between two of yours flip to your colour. A board that looks lost can turn around in the last few moves.

How to play

  1. The game starts with four discs in the middle. Black moves first.
  2. Place a disc on a marked square. A move must trap at least one rival disc in a straight line between the new disc and another of yours.
  3. All trapped discs flip to your colour, in every direction at once.
  4. If you have no legal move your turn is skipped. The game ends when neither player can move.

Scoring

Your score is the number of discs showing your colour at the end.

Who wins

The player with the most discs at the end wins. Equal counts are a draw.

Tips

  • Corners can never be flipped. Try to take them.
  • Avoid the squares next to an empty corner: they hand the corner to the other player.
  • Early on, having fewer discs is often better: it leaves the other player fewer moves.

Questions

What if I can't move?

Your turn is skipped automatically, and you play again as soon as you have a legal move.

Who goes first?

Black, played by the first player at the table.
