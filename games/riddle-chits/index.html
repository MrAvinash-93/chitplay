Riddle Chits turns a box of folded riddles into a quick game. One riddle opens at a time and everyone picks an answer in secret from four options. Stuck? Take a hint, but it costs you a point.

Ways to play Riddle Chits

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 10 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. A riddle opens on the chit for everyone to read.
  2. Pick the answer you think is right. Nobody sees your choice until all have answered.
  3. Not sure? Tap for a hint before you answer. It lowers your points for that riddle.
  4. The answer and everyone's choices are then shown, and the next chit opens.

Scoring

A right answer earns 2 points, or 1 if you took the hint. A wrong answer earns nothing.

Who wins

The player with the most points after the last riddle wins.

Tips

  • Read every word: many riddles are about wordplay.
  • Think of everyday objects first.

Questions

Are the riddles for all ages?

Yes. They're classic family-friendly riddles.

Can two people play?

Yes, and computer players can make the table bigger.

How do you play Riddle Chits online with friends?

Open Riddle Chits on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Riddle Chits without downloading anything?

Yes. Riddle Chits runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Riddle Chits and play as a guest.

How many players can play Riddle Chits?

Riddle Chits on ChitPlay is for 2 to 10 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Riddle Chits free to play?

Yes, Riddle Chits is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Riddle Chits against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
