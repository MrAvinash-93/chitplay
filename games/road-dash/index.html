Road Dash puts you at the handlebars of an auto-rickshaw on the busiest road in town. You only have to do one thing: change lanes. But the traffic gets thicker and the road gets faster, and sooner or later a cow will be standing exactly where you need to go.

Ways to play Road Dash

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Press left or right (drag the stick, A and D, or the arrow keys) to change lanes.
  2. Dodge the cows, potholes, buses and other rickshaws.
  3. Collect coins for extra points.
  4. The road gets faster the longer you last, and every 100 or so points is a new level with new traffic. One crash ends your run.

Scoring

You score for the distance travelled, and 5 for each coin.

Who wins

Alone, beat your own best score. With others, the highest score wins.

Levels

  1. Village road: Only cows and potholes, one at a time. Learn the lanes.
  2. Town road: Buses join the traffic, and now and then two lanes are blocked at once.
  3. Highway: Two things in the way more often, and other rickshaws on the road.
  4. Rush hour (level 4 and up): Green rickshaws race towards you faster than the rest of the traffic. Every level after this is a little busier.
  5. Coin rush: Every new level opens with a few seconds of coins in a zigzag line: 5 points each.

Tips

  • Stay in the middle lanes: they have two ways out.
  • Don't chase every coin.
  • Look ahead down the lane you are about to enter.

Questions

Does the road ever end?

No. It keeps getting faster, and every level brings new traffic: buses, then two at a time, then rickshaws racing at you.

Can friends race me?

Yes, up to four can share the same road, on one screen or on their own phones.

How do you play Road Dash online with friends?

Open Road Dash on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Road Dash without downloading anything?

Yes. Road Dash runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Road Dash and play as a guest.

How many players can play Road Dash?

Road Dash on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Road Dash free to play?

Yes, Road Dash is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Road Dash against the computer?

Yes. You can play Road Dash on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
