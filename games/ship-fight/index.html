Ship Fight: play free online and learn the rules

  • 👥 2 players
  • ⏱ 2–5 minutes
  • 👤 guest play
  • 🌍 online opponents
  • 🤖 computer players
  • 📶 friends nearby
  • 🆓 free, no sign-up

Ship Fight is a two-player battle made for sitting side by side. Each ship stays in its own half of the sea and its cannon fires across it on its own, again and again. You only steer: dodge the incoming shots, line yours up, use the rocks as cover and grab the crates. Every round takes under a minute.

Ways to play Ship Fight

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 📶 Friends nearby Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Move your ship anywhere in your half of the sea. Its cannon fires across at the other ship by itself, every second or so.
  2. Each ship can take five hits. Rocks stop shots, so use them as cover.
  3. Crates appear in the middle: a heart repairs your ship, and the cannon crate gives five triple shots.
  4. If a round goes on too long a storm wears the ships down, starting with whichever has less health.

Scoring

Sinking the other ship wins the round.

Who wins

The first to win three rounds takes the match.

Tips

  • Keep moving: a ship standing still is an easy target.
  • Fire where the other ship is going, not where it is.
  • A crate is worth a risk, but the other ship is watching it too.

Questions

How do two people play?

On one keyboard, one player uses W A S D and Space and the other the arrows and Enter. Gamepads work too, one each.

Can I play alone?

Yes, against a computer player that makes mistakes just as people do.
