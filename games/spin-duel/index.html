Spin Duel is a head-to-head spinning-top battle inspired by schoolyard games. Both players get three seconds to flick a top into the arena. A faster swipe launches harder; the tops collide, lose spin and can be knocked out. No special hardware or copyrighted characters: just your own tops, arena and timing.

Ways to play Spin Duel

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Swipe quickly toward your rival within the three-second launch window. The Launch button is an accessible alternative.
  2. A longer, faster swipe gives your top more launch power. Make one launch per round.
  3. After the timer, the tops spin and collide automatically. Keep your top in the arena and spinning.
  4. The last top spinning wins the round. If both stop together, the round is a draw.
  5. First to three round wins takes the match. If both players miss three rounds, the match ends tied.

Scoring

Win a round by knocking the other top out or outlasting its spin. A match is first to three round wins.

Who wins

Play solo against a bot, on one screen, nearby or online. Earn coins through play and use them to unlock top and arena styles.

Tips

  • Aim straight toward the other top for an early collision.
  • A fast launch hits hard, but a controlled launch can keep you in the arena longer.
  • Watch the top’s spin and position; collisions can change both.

Questions

How do I launch?

Swipe on the arena or the swipe pad within three seconds. Faster swipes launch with more power.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Start a two-player game and the computer fills the other seat.

Do styles affect power?

No. Unlockable tops and arenas are visual styles only.

Open Spin Duel on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Spin Duel without downloading anything?

Yes. Spin Duel runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Spin Duel and play as a guest.

How many players can play Spin Duel?

Spin Duel on ChitPlay is for 2 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.

Is Spin Duel free to play?

Yes, Spin Duel is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Spin Duel against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play and ChitPlay's computer players take the other seats, at an easy, normal or hard level.
