Truth or Dare is the classic party game: on your turn, answer a question honestly or do a small challenge. ChitPlay's prompts suit every age: nothing dangerous, rude or embarrassing, and nothing about private matters. It's at its best with people in the same room. Nothing is typed, so nothing personal is shared at an online table.

Ways to play Truth or Dare

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 10 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 📶 Friends nearby Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. On your turn, choose Truth or Dare.
  2. Read the prompt, then answer it out loud or do it.
  3. Tap Done for a point, or tap Skip. There is never a penalty for skipping.
  4. The turn moves to the next player.

Scoring

Every truth told and dare done earns 1 point. Skipping costs nothing.

Who wins

The player with the most points after the last turn wins, though the real point is the laughs.

Tips

  • Agree before you start that anyone can skip anything.
  • Mix truths and dares to keep the game moving.

Questions

Are the prompts suitable for everyone?

Yes. They're harmless questions and light challenges such as impressions, tongue twisters or a short dance.

Can I skip a prompt?

Yes, always, and it never costs points.
