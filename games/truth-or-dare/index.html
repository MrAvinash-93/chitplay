Truth or Dare: how to play, free on one phone

  • 👥 2–15 players
  • ⏱ 10–30 minutes
  • 📱 one phone, pass it round
  • 🆓 free, no sign-up

Truth or Dare is the classic party game: on your turn, choose to answer a question honestly or to do a small challenge. ChitPlay's prompts are written to be fun for every age: no dangerous, rude or embarrassing dares, and nothing about private matters.

▶ Play Truth or Dare now

How to play

  1. Add the players and choose how many turns each.
  2. On your turn, choose Truth or Dare.
  3. Read the prompt aloud and answer it or do it.
  4. Not comfortable? Press Skip: there is never a penalty for skipping.
  5. The phone moves to the next player.

Scoring

Every truth answered and every dare done earns 1 point. Skipping costs nothing.

Who wins

The player with the most points after the last turn wins, but the real point is the laughs.

Tips

  • Agree before you start that anyone can skip anything.
  • Mix truths and dares to keep the game moving.

Questions

Is ChitPlay's Truth or Dare safe for children?

The prompts are written for every age: harmless questions and light challenges like impressions, tongue twisters or a short dance.

Can I skip a prompt?

Yes, always, and skipping never costs points.
