Turbo Tunnel is ChitPlay's own 3D car racing game. Your car drives on the whole inside of a round road tunnel: the floor, the walls and even the roof. Steer round the tunnel to get past cones, barriers and traffic. Every level brings a new danger, from cars coming the wrong way to turning laser gates and rocks dropping from the roof, and the tunnel keeps getting faster. Race alone for a high score, or race friends on their own phones, nearby or online, and see who lasts longest.

Ways to play Turbo Tunnel

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Drag the stick left or right, or use A and D, the arrow keys or a gamepad, to steer round the inside of the tunnel.
  2. Cones, barriers, cars, trucks and rocks end your run if you touch them. The next one your car is lined up to hit glows red underneath.
  3. Every row of the tunnel always has a clear way through that you can reach in time. Faint green arrows on the road show it.
  4. Coins are worth 10 points. A blue nitro pad doubles your distance points for three seconds. A green shield takes one hit for you.
  5. Oil does not stop you, but the car slides and answers the wheel slowly for a moment.
  6. Passing something by a whisker is a close call: 5 bonus points.

Scoring

Points for the distance you drive (double on nitro), 10 for each coin and 5 for each close call. Medals at 100, 300, 700 and 1500 points.

Who wins

Alone, beat your own best score. With friends, everyone races the same tunnel at once: the last car still driving wins, and if everyone crashes the highest score wins.

Levels

  1. City tunnel: Cones and striped barriers.
  2. Traffic: Slower cars in your way, and patches of oil.
  3. Wrong way: Cars and trucks coming straight at you.
  4. Laser gates: Rings of laser with one gap that turn as you come at them. Steer for where the gap will be.
  5. Cave-in: Rocks drop from the roof. A red ring shows where each one will land.
  6. Turbo night (level 6 and up): Everything at once, closer together and faster.
  7. Coin ring: Every new level opens with a ring of coins all the way round.

Tips

  • Look two rows ahead, not just at the next one: the nearest gap is not always the one that leads on.
  • Small, early moves beat big late swerves.
  • Follow the coins: they always lead onto the sure way through.
  • Save the shield for the laser gates: keep it by not touching anything.

Questions

Is it hard?

It starts slowly with wide gaps. Every row always has a way through, so a crash is never bad luck.

Can I race my friends?

Yes. Up to four cars race the same tunnel: on one screen, on your own phones with Nearby, or online.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, for free.

Is it like other tunnel games?

It is our own idea: real cars and traffic driving round the whole inside of a tunnel, with a new danger every level.

How do you play Turbo Tunnel online with friends?

Open Turbo Tunnel on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Turbo Tunnel without downloading anything?

Yes. Turbo Tunnel runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Turbo Tunnel and play as a guest.

How many players can play Turbo Tunnel?

Turbo Tunnel on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Turbo Tunnel free to play?

Yes, Turbo Tunnel is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Turbo Tunnel against the computer?

Yes. You can play Turbo Tunnel on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
