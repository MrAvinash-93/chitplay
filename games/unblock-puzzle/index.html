Unblock Puzzle: play free online and learn the rules

Unblock Puzzle is a sliding-block logic game. An auto-rickshaw is stuck in a packed 6 × 6 parking lot, and the gate is on the right. Cars and trucks only move forwards and backwards, never sideways, so every move matters. Clear a path and drive the rickshaw out. A game has five lots, from easy to hard, and every lot has been solved by computer, so each one can be done and the fewest moves it takes is shown. Play alone, or race friends on the same lots.

Ways to play Unblock Puzzle

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Each lot shows the rickshaw (green, yellow canopy), the vehicles around it and the gate on the right of its row.
  2. Drag a vehicle with your finger or the mouse to slide it. It goes as far as you drag, up to the first thing in its way.
  3. Or use the arrows to pick a vehicle, press Hold, and slide it with the arrows. Press Hold again to let go.
  4. When nothing is between the rickshaw and the gate, slide it right and it drives out. You have two minutes per lot.

Scoring

Getting out scores 50, plus up to 50 more for doing it in few moves: 5 points less for each move over the best. With friends, the first one out of each lot gets 20 more. Medals at 200, 300, 400 and 470 points.

Who wins

After five lots, the most points wins.

Tips

  • Start from the gate: see what blocks the rickshaw, then what blocks that.
  • Trucks are three squares long and are often the key.
  • One long slide counts as one move, so slide as far as you need in one go.

Questions

Can every lot be solved?

Yes. Every lot was solved by computer before it went in, and the fewest moves it takes is shown above your move count.

What counts as a move?

Sliding one vehicle, however far it goes, is one move.

Yes. Unblock Puzzle runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Unblock Puzzle and play as a guest.

Unblock Puzzle on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Can I play Unblock Puzzle against the computer?

Yes. You can play Unblock Puzzle on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
