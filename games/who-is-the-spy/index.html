Who Is the Spy?: how to play, free on one phone

  • 👥 4–10 players
  • ⏱ 10–20 minutes
  • 📱 one phone, pass it round
  • 🆓 free, no sign-up

Who Is the Spy is a word game of bluffing for a group in the same room. Everybody secretly gets the same word, except one player, the spy, who gets a similar but different word. Nobody knows who the spy is, not even the spy. Give a clue, listen to everyone else's, and work out who doesn't quite fit.

▶ Play Who Is the Spy? now

How to play

  1. Add the players and choose a word category (food, places, things, animals or mixed).
  2. Pass the phone around: each player privately sees their secret word, then hides it.
  3. Starting with a random player, everyone gives one short clue about their word without saying it.
  4. Talk it over, then vote together for the player you think is the spy.
  5. If you caught the spy, the spy gets one last chance: guess the group's word from four options.

Scoring

If the spy is not caught, the spy scores 3. If the spy is caught and then guesses the group's word, the spy scores 2; if the guess is wrong, every other player scores 1. With eight or more players there are two spies.

Who wins

After the last round the player with the most points wins.

Tips

  • Clues that are too exact help the spy guess the word; clues that are too vague make you look like the spy.
  • If your word seems slightly different from everyone else's clues, you might be the spy: blend in.

Questions

How many players do you need for Who Is the Spy?

At least four, and up to ten. With eight or more players ChitPlay picks two spies.

Does the spy know they are the spy?

No. The spy simply gets a different word, so they have to notice the difference from the other clues.
