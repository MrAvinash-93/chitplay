Who Is the Spy is a word game of bluffing. Everyone secretly gets the same word, except one player, the spy, who gets a similar but different word. Nobody knows who the spy is, not even the spy. In ChitPlay, clues are played as cards, so it works with computer players and on tables with strangers too.

Ways to play Who Is the Spy?

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 4 to 10 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Each player privately reads their secret word.
  2. Going round the table, each player plays one clue card for their word.
  3. Read the clues, then everyone votes in secret for the player they suspect.
  4. The player with the most votes is accused. A tied vote means nobody is, and the spy gets away.
  5. If the spy is caught, they get one guess at the others' word from four options.

Scoring

A spy who escapes scores 3. A caught spy who guesses the word scores 2; if the guess is wrong, everyone else scores 1. With eight or more players there are two spies.

Who wins

After three rounds, the player with the most points wins.

Tips

  • Your word may be the odd one out. Read the others' clues before you trust it.
  • Clues that fit both words of a likely pair keep you safe.
  • If a clue doesn't fit your word, that player may be the spy, or you may be.

Questions

Does the spy know they are the spy?

No. The spy simply has a different word, and has to notice from the others' clues.

How many players are needed?

At least four. Computer players can fill the seats.
