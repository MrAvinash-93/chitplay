Word Guess is a quick vocabulary race. Each word comes with a clue, like Fruit, Animal or Sport, and starts as blank tiles with one letter showing. Every second and a half another letter appears. Guess the whole word as soon as you are sure: the more letters still hidden, the more it is worth. But you get only one guess per word, so jumping in too early can cost you the round. Eight words a game, alone or against friends.

Ways to play Word Guess

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away, no account and no sign-up. Your best score is kept on your device.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 🎮 Two on one screen Sit side by side: one player on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys, or a gamepad each.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. You see a clue (like Fruit or Sport) and the word as blank tiles, with one letter showing.
  2. Another letter shows every second and a half. The last letter never shows, so you always have to guess.
  3. Type the whole word on the letters keypad (or your keyboard) and press ✓. You get one guess per word.
  4. Each phone times its own player, and friends' phones only ever see the letters already showing, never the word.

Scoring

A right guess scores 10, plus 10 for every letter still hidden when you sent it. With friends, the quickest right guess on each word earns 10 more. A wrong guess scores nothing for that word. Medals at 80, 160, 240 and 320 points.

Who wins

After eight words, the player with the most points wins.

Tips

  • Think of words that fit both the clue and the number of tiles.
  • The first letter or the last two letters often give the word away.
  • If two words still fit, wait for one more letter.

Questions

Which language are the words in?

The words are everyday English words, such as fruits, animals and things at home, so the game is also good English vocabulary practice.

What happens if I guess wrong?

You score nothing for that word and wait for the next one. There is only one guess per word, so guessing too early is a risk.

How do you play Word Guess online with friends?

Open Word Guess on ChitPlay and choose Play with Friends. One phone opens a room and gets a 6-digit code; your friends type the code or scan the QR code on their own phones and join. No sign-up is needed.

Can I play Word Guess without downloading anything?

Yes. Word Guess runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Open www.chitplay.com, pick Word Guess and play as a guest.

How many players can play Word Guess?

Word Guess on ChitPlay is for 1 to 4 players. You can play alone, or with friends and family.

Is Word Guess free to play?

Yes, Word Guess is completely free on ChitPlay. There is no real money, betting or prizes, and points have no value outside the game.

Can I play Word Guess against the computer?

Yes. You can play Word Guess on your own and try to beat your best score, or race against computer players.
