Yacht is a traditional dice game from the early 1900s: five dice, up to three rolls a turn, and twelve categories to fill. Each category can be used only once, so the skill lies in deciding when to take a low score and when to gamble on another roll. There is no betting: the points are only there to keep score.

Ways to play Yacht

  • 👤 Guest play Start straight away against computer players, no account and no sign-up. Choose 2 to 4 players.
  • 🌍 Play online Get matched with real players from around the world. If nobody is free within a few seconds, computer players take the empty seats so you are never left waiting.
  • 👫 Play with friends Open a room, and friends join from their own phones with a 6-digit code or by scanning a QR code. Works on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet.
  • 📱 One phone Pass one phone around the table; secrets stay hidden until it is your turn.
  • 👀 Watch Let the computer players show you how the game goes before you jump in.

How to play

  1. Roll all five dice. Tap any you want to keep, then roll the rest. You can do this up to three rolls a turn.
  2. After any roll, you may stop and score in one category you haven't used yet.
  3. Ones to Sixes score the total of that number. Choice scores the total of all the dice.
  4. Four of a kind scores the total of those four dice. Full house (three of one and two of another) scores the total of all five.
  5. Little straight (1–5) and Big straight (2–6) score 30. Yacht (all five alike) scores 50.
  6. If nothing fits you must still pick a category, even if it scores 0.

Scoring

Your total is the sum of all twelve categories.

Who wins

After twelve turns each, the highest total wins.

House rules you can switch on

  • Upper bonus: Score 63 or more in Ones to Sixes together (that is three of each number) and you get 35 extra points at the end.

Tips

  • Use Choice as a safety net for a poor roll.
  • A 0 in Yacht early on is often better than wasting a good Sixes.
  • With three of a kind, keep them and roll the other two: that can make a Four of a kind, a Full house or even a Yacht.

Questions

Is Yacht a gambling game?

No. There is nothing to bet, buy or win in ChitPlay. The points have no value outside the game.

How many categories are there?

Twelve, and you fill one each turn.

Yacht on ChitPlay is for 2 to 4 players. Computer players fill any empty seats, so you can always start a game.
