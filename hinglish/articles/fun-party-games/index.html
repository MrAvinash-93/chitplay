Mazedaar party games

Update: 2026-11-14

Party ka game samjhaane mein aasaan, shuru karne mein jaldi aur kamra goonjaane laayak hona chahiye. Sabse achhe games har kisi ko ek pal ke liye spotlight mein laate hain, aur kisi ke bore hone se pehle aage badh jaate hain.

Yahan birthday, get-together aur tyohaaron ke liye party games hain, chhote aur bade group ke liye, aamne-saamne ya sabke phone pe.

Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Dumb Charades (Dum Sharaz)

    Ek player bina bole kisi film, kitaab ya gaane ka naam acting karke batata hai aur uski team andaaza lagati hai. Pehle se "film", "gaana" aur shabdon ki ginti ke ishaare tay kar lo.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Do sach aur ek jhooth

    Har player apne baare mein teen baatein bolta hai; unmein se ek banawati hoti hai. Baaki vote karte hain ki jhooth kaunsa hai.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Antakshari

    Kisi bhi gaane ki kuch lines gao; agli team ko usi akshar se naya gaana shuru karna hai jis pe tumhara gaana khatam hua. Gaana na soojhe to team round haar jaati hai.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Garam-Thanda

    Ek player bahar rukta hai aur koi chhoti cheez chhupa di jaati hai. Lautne pe sab bolte hain "garam" ya "thanda", jaise-jaise woh paas ya door jaata hai.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: ek chhoti cheez

Tips

  • Jab log aa hi rahe hon, tab ek chhota aur aasaan game shuru karo.
  • Who Is the Spy jaise chhupe kirdaar waale games har round ke saath behtar hote hain.
  • Sazaayein dostana rakho, taaki haarne pe kisi ko bura na lage.
