  • Angoothe ki kushti

    Saamne waale se ungliyan phansao aur uska angootha teen ginne tak dabaa ke rakhne ki koshish karo.

    👥 2 players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Patthar, Kaagaz, Kainchi

    Teen tak gino aur haath se ishaara dikhao: patthar kainchi todta hai, kainchi kaagaz kaatti hai, kaagaz patthar ko lapet leta hai. Paanch mein se teen jeetne waala winner.

  • Bees sawaal

    Ek player kisi insaan, jaanwar ya cheez ke baare mein sochta hai. Baaki zyada se zyada bees haan-ya-na waale sawaal pooch ke pata lagate hain ki woh kya hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Har game mein pehli chaal badlo, kyunki pehli chaal aksar fayda deti hai.
  • Teen mein se do jeetne ka rule rakho, taaki kismat waali jeet ka hisaab barabar ho.
  • Lambe game ke baad ek chhota game khelo, maza bana rehta hai.
