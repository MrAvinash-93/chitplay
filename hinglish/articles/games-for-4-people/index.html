Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Dumb Charades (Dum Sharaz)

    Ek player bina bole kisi film, kitaab ya gaane ka naam acting karke batata hai aur uski team andaaza lagati hai. Pehle se "film", "gaana" aur shabdon ki ginti ke ishaare tay kar lo.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Naam, Jagah, Jaanwar, Cheez

    Ek akshar chuno. Sab usi akshar se ek naam, ek jagah, ek jaanwar aur ek cheez jaldi se likhte hain. Alag jawaabon pe zyada points.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kaagaz aur pen

  • Kahaani ki chain

    Ek insaan ek line se kahaani shuru karta hai; har player agli line jodta hai. Jitne ajeeb mod, utni mazedaar kahaani.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Call Break jaise haath jeetne waale taash ke games theek chaar logon mein sabse achhe hain.
  • Jodiyon mein khel ke dekho: partners ek-doosre ke saamne baithein.
  • Agar koi beech mein chala jaaye, to ChitPlay uski jagah computer player bitha deta hai.

Sawaal-jawaab

4 logon ke liye sabse achha game kaunsa hai?

Raja Rani Chor Police (chaar chit waala game), Call Break aur Ludo chaar logon ke liye hi bane hain, aur teeno ChitPlay pe free hain.

Chaar log bina taash ya board ke kya khelein?

Do teams mein Dumb Charades, Kahaani ki chain aur Naam, Jagah, Jaanwar, Cheez chaar logon ke liye achhe hain.
