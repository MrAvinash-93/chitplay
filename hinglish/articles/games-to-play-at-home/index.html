Update: 2026-11-14

Ghar pe ek achha game ek normal shaam ko yaadgaar bana deta hai. Iske liye almaari bhar ke dabbe nahi chahiye: kuch sabse achhe games ke liye bas kamre mein maujood log kaafi hain, aur baaki phone pe kuch tap ki doori pe hain.

Neeche aise games hain jo drawing room mein, baarish ki dopahar mein ya khaane ke baad chalte hain, ek insaan se poore ghar tak ke liye. Kitne log hain aur kitni energy bachi hai, uske hisaab se chuno.

Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Dumb Charades (Dum Sharaz)

    Ek player bina bole kisi film, kitaab ya gaane ka naam acting karke batata hai aur uski team andaaza lagati hai. Pehle se "film", "gaana" aur shabdon ki ginti ke ishaare tay kar lo.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Antakshari

    Kisi bhi gaane ki kuch lines gao; agli team ko usi akshar se naya gaana shuru karna hai jis pe tumhara gaana khatam hua. Gaana na soojhe to team round haar jaati hai.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Garam-Thanda

    Ek player bahar rukta hai aur koi chhoti cheez chhupa di jaati hai. Lautne pe sab bolte hain "garam" ya "thanda", jaise-jaise woh paas ya door jaata hai.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: ek chhoti cheez

  • Kahaani ki chain

    Ek insaan ek line se kahaani shuru karta hai; har player agli line jodta hai. Jitne ajeeb mod, utni mazedaar kahaani.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Ek bhaag-daud waala aur ek shaant game milao, taaki har kisi ko chamakne ka mauka mile.
  • Rounds chhote rakho: paanch-das minute mein sabka interest bana rehta hai.
  • Pehla game sabse chhote ya naye player ko chunne do.

Sawaal-jawaab
