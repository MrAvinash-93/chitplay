School mein khelne waale games

Update: 2026-11-14

Ab tak ke kuch sabse achhe games school ki copy ke aakhri panne pe bane the. Woh jaldi hote hain, lagbhag kuch nahi chahiye, aur teacher ke aate hi ek pal mein chhup jaate hain.

Ye games free period, break ya class activity ke liye sahi hain. Kai games spelling, ginti ya yaaddasht mein bhi madad karte hain, isliye teacher bhi inhe use kar sakte hain.

Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Naam, Jagah, Jaanwar, Cheez

    Ek akshar chuno. Sab usi akshar se ek naam, ek jagah, ek jaanwar aur ek cheez jaldi se likhte hain. Alag jawaabon pe zyada points.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kaagaz aur pen

  • Hangman

    Ek player secret shabd ke liye dash banata hai. Baaki akshar batate hain; har galat akshar pe drawing mein ek line judti hai. Drawing poori hone se pehle shabd batao.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kaagaz aur pen

  • Shabd ki chain

    Ek shabd bolo; agla player aisa shabd bole jo uske aakhri akshar se shuru ho. Shabd dohraana mana hai aur paanch second ka time hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Patthar, Kaagaz, Kainchi

    Teen tak gino aur haath se ishaara dikhao: patthar kainchi todta hai, kainchi kaagaz kaatti hai, kaagaz patthar ko lapet leta hai. Paanch mein se teen jeetne waala winner.

    👥 2 players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Dots and Boxes aur Hangman jaise kaagaz ke games copy ke ek panne pe aa jaate hain.
  • Poori class ke liye teams banao aur board pe score likho.
  • Number Memory jaise dimaagi games padhai se pehle achha warm-up hain.

Sawaal-jawaab

Class mein kaagaz se kaunse games khel sakte hain?

Chaar chit waala game (Raja Rani Chor Police), Dots and Boxes, Tic-Tac-Toe, Hangman, Bingo aur Naam, Jagah, Jaanwar, Cheez, sabke liye bas kaagaz aur pen chahiye.

Kya school mein khelne ke liye seekhne waale games hain?

Haan. Word Guess aur Fast Typing English words ki practice karaate hain, Number Memory yaaddasht badhaata hai, aur Naam, Jagah, Jaanwar, Cheez vocabulary badhaata hai.
