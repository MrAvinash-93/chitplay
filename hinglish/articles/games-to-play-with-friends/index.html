Doston ke saath khelne waale games

Update: 2026-11-14

Doston ke saath sabse achhe games wahi hain jinmein log baatein karein, hansein aur ek-doosre pe ilzaam lagayein. Bluff waale games, chhote muqable aur team waale games, sab chalte hain, chahe ek kamre mein ho ya sab apne-apne phone pe.

Yahan do se das doston ke liye games hain, saath ho tab ke liye bhi aur door ho tab ke liye bhi.

Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Do sach aur ek jhooth

    Har player apne baare mein teen baatein bolta hai; unmein se ek banawati hoti hai. Baaki vote karte hain ki jhooth kaunsa hai.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Kya chunoge?

    Do mazedaar ya mushkil options mein se ek chunne ko kaho, jaise "Sirf gaa ke bologe ya sirf tukbandi mein?", aur har koi apna jawab samjhata hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Dumb Charades (Dum Sharaz)

    Ek player bina bole kisi film, kitaab ya gaane ka naam acting karke batata hai aur uski team andaaza lagati hai. Pehle se "film", "gaana" aur shabdon ki ginti ke ishaare tay kar lo.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Bluff waale games tab sabse achhe hain jab sab ek-doosre ko jaante hon: purani aadatein clue ban jaati hain.
  • Shuru karne se pehle last place ke liye ek halki-phulki saza tay karo.
  • Online khelte waqt saath mein voice ya video call rakho.

Sawaal-jawaab

Dost online free mein kaunse games khel sakte hain?

ChitPlay pe Raja Rani Chor Police, Who Is the Spy, Ludo, Call Break, Bingo aur bahut kuch browser mein free hai. Ek insaan room banaa ke code ya link bhejta hai.

Doston ke bade group ke liye kaunsa game achha hai?

Who Is the Spy aur Werewolf paanch se das logon ke liye achhe hain; Dumb Charades kitne bhi logon mein do teams banaa ke khel sakte ho.
