Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Patthar, Kaagaz, Kainchi

    Teen tak gino aur haath se ishaara dikhao: patthar kainchi todta hai, kainchi kaagaz kaatti hai, kaagaz patthar ko lapet leta hai. Paanch mein se teen jeetne waala winner.

    👥 2 players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Bees sawaal

    Ek player kisi insaan, jaanwar ya cheez ke baare mein sochta hai. Baaki zyada se zyada bees haan-ya-na waale sawaal pooch ke pata lagate hain ki woh kya hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • I Spy (Meri nazar mein)

    Bolo "Meri nazar mein kuch hai jo … se shuru hota hai" aur ek akshar batao. Sab aas-paas dekh ke cheez ka andaaza lagate hain. Sahi batane waala agli cheez chunta hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Shabd ki chain

    Ek shabd bolo; agla player aisa shabd bole jo uske aakhri akshar se shuru ho. Shabd dohraana mana hai aur paanch second ka time hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Kya chunoge?

    Do mazedaar ya mushkil options mein se ek chunne ko kaho, jaise "Sirf gaa ke bologe ya sirf tukbandi mein?", aur har koi apna jawab samjhata hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Simon says

    Leader kaam batata hai. Kaam tabhi karo jab order "Simon says" se shuru ho; bina iske hilne waala bahar.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Angoothe ki kushti

    Saamne waale se ungliyan phansao aur uska angootha teen ginne tak dabaa ke rakhne ki koshish karo.

    👥 2 players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Kahaani ki chain

    Ek insaan ek line se kahaani shuru karta hai; har player agli line jodta hai. Jitne ajeeb mod, utni mazedaar kahaani.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Shabd aur andaaze waale games car ya train ke safar ke liye badhiya hain.
  • Jaise-jaise sab behtar hon, rules sakht karo: kam time, mushkil akshar.
  • Agar kisi ke paas phone hai, to ChitPlay ke games ke liye na download chahiye, na sign-up.

Sawaal-jawaab

Bina kuch ke kaunse games khel sakte hain?

Patthar-Kaagaz-Kainchi, Bees sawaal, I Spy, Shabd ki chain, Kya chunoge, Simon says aur Angoothe ki kushti ke liye kuch nahi chahiye.
