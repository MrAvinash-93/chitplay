Poori family ke liye indoor games

Update: 2026-11-14

Family ke sabse achhe games woh hain jinmein dada-dadi aur teenager ke jeetne ka barabar mauka ho. Kismat, bluff aur masti sabko barabari pe le aate hain, jo sirf skill waale games nahi kar paate.

Ye indoor games family ki shaam, tyohaaron aur chhuttiyon ke liye hain, jab har umar ke log ek hi table pe hon, ya ek hi phone ke aas-paas.

Kahin bhi khele jaane waale purane games

  • Antakshari

    Kisi bhi gaane ki kuch lines gao; agli team ko usi akshar se naya gaana shuru karna hai jis pe tumhara gaana khatam hua. Gaana na soojhe to team round haar jaati hai.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Dumb Charades (Dum Sharaz)

    Ek player bina bole kisi film, kitaab ya gaane ka naam acting karke batata hai aur uski team andaaza lagati hai. Pehle se "film", "gaana" aur shabdon ki ginti ke ishaare tay kar lo.

    👥 4+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • Simon says

    Leader kaam batata hai. Kaam tabhi karo jab order "Simon says" se shuru ho; bina iske hilne waala bahar.

    👥 3+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

  • I Spy (Meri nazar mein)

    Bolo "Meri nazar mein kuch hai jo … se shuru hota hai" aur ek akshar batao. Sab aas-paas dekh ke cheez ka andaaza lagate hain. Sahi batane waala agli cheez chunta hai.

    👥 2+ players · Zaroorat: kuch nahi

Tips

  • Aise games chuno jinmein kismat ka bhi hissa ho, taaki chhote bhi jeet sakein.
  • Sabse chhote ko sabse bade ke saath team mein rakho.
  • Family ka score sheet rakho aur chhuttiyon ke end mein champion chuno.
