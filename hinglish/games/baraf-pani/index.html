Baraf Pani (Baraf Pani)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 3–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Tag hone par jam jao; teammate ko chhoo kar use phir se game mein lao.

English name: Baraf Pani

Kaise khelein

  1. Baraf Pani teen ya chaar log khelte hain: ek IT aur baaki bhaagne wale. Khaali jagah computer bharta hai, aur IT har match badalta hai.
  2. IT maidan ke beech teen tak ginta hai aur baaki bhaagte hain. Phir pakdam-pakdai shuru.
  3. Joystick ya arrow keys se chalo. Sprint se thodi der tez bhaago; second bhar mein saans phool jaati hai aur thoda chalna padta hai.
  4. IT ne chhua to woh jam gaya (baraf!). Azaad dost ne jame hue ko chhua to woh phir azaad (pani!).
  5. Sab ek saath jam gaye to IT jeeta. Time khatam hone tak ek bhi azaad raha to bhaagne wale jeete.

Points kaise milte hain

Team ki tarah jeeta jaata hai: IT sabko jamaa ke, bhaagne wale ek ko bhi time tak azaad rakh ke. Jeetne walon ko 1 point.

Kaun jeetta hai

Computer runners ke saath akele, ek screen pe dost, paas mein ya online khelo. Khaali seat bot bharte hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya jama hua player chal sakta hai?

Nahi. Use pighlaane ke liye kisi saathi ko chhoona padta hai.

Do players kyun nahi?

Baraf Pani mein pighlaane wala saathi chahiye. Do mein pehla chhoona hi game khatam kar deta, isliye ChitPlay kam se kam teen ko bithata hai aur kami computer poori karta hai.

Kya main bots ke saath khel sakta hoon?

Haan. Khaali seatein computer players bhar dete hain.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
