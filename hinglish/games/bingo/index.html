Bingo (Bingo)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

1 se 25 tak ka tumhara apna 5 × 5 card. Baari-baari number bolo; paanch lines pehle poori karne waala bole BINGO.

English name: Bingo

Kaise khelein

  1. Har player ko 1 se 25 tak alag order waala card milta hai. Tumhara card sirf tum dekh sakte ho.
  2. Apni baari pe 1 se 25 ke beech aisa number type karo jo abhi tak na bola gaya ho, aur ✓ dabao. 15 second hain, warna tumhari jagah koi number bol diya jaata hai.
  3. Bola gaya number sabke card pe kat jaata hai, tumhare aur doston ke bhi.
  4. Har poori row, column ya tirchhi line ek line hai aur B-I-N-G-O ka agla akshar jalaati hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Upar ki patti dikhati hai ki har player ke BINGO ke kitne akshar jale hain. Ek card pe baarah lines ban sakti hain: paanch rows, paanch columns aur do tirchhi.

Kaun jeetta hai

Paanch lines pehle poori karne waala jeetta hai. Agar ek hi number se do card saath poore hon to zyada lines waala jeetta hai; phir bhi barabar ho to jisne number bola woh jeetta hai.

Kya dost mera card dekh sakte hain?

Nahi. Har phone ko sirf uska apna card milta hai; baaki game khatam hone tak chhupe rehte hain.

Agar maine pehle se bola gaya number type kar diya to?

Kuch nahi jaata: game bata deta hai aur time khatam hone se pehle tum doosra number type kar sakte ho.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
