Bull Fight (Bull Fight)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Do bail, ek ret ka ghera. Daud kar takkar maaro aur doosre ko bahar dhakelo.

English name: Bull Fight

Kaise khelein

  1. Stick drag karo, ya W A S D, arrow keys ya gamepad se apna bail chalao.
  2. Charge dabao aage daudne ke liye. Doosra bail lagbhag saamne ho to charge seedha uski taraf jaata hai. Phir bar pe CHARGE READY aane ka intezaar karo.
  3. Har bail ke neeche ring aur teer batate hain ki kiska hai aur kis taraf dekh raha hai. Kinare pe khur ret pakad lete hain, isliye dhakka lage tabhi bahar jaoge; paas ho to kinara laal chamakta hai aur EDGE! dikhta hai.
  4. Charge karta bail doosre se takraye to use uda deta hai aur thodi der chakkar aate hain.
  5. Jo safed line ke bahar dhakela gaya woh round haara. Thodi der baad ring chhoti hone lagti hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Doosre bail ko bahar dhakelo, round jeeto.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo pehle teen round jeete, match uska.

Kya kisi ko chot lagti hai?

Nahi. Ye dhakka dene ka game hai, bail bas ek pal ke liye ladkhadaate hain.

Do log kaise khelein?

Ek keyboard pe ek player W A S D aur Space, doosra arrows aur Enter chalaye. Gamepad aur online bhi chalta hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
