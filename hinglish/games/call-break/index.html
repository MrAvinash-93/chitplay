Call Break (Call Break)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 4 players
  • Card games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Apne haath bolo, phir banao. Hukum hamesha trump hota hai.

English name: Call Break

Kaise khelein

  1. Saare 52 patte baante jaate hain, har player ko 13.
  2. Dealer ke baad wale se shuru karke har player 1 se 8 tak haath (trick) bolta hai.
  3. Pehla player koi bhi patta chalta hai. Ghoomte hue har player ko wahi rang chalna hai, aur ho sake to ab tak ke sabse bade se bada.
  4. Wo rang na ho to hukum (spade) chalna padega, aur ho sake to sabse bade hukum se bada. Hukum bhi na ho to kuch bhi.
  5. Sabse bada hukum haath jeetta hai; hukum na ho to chale gaye rang ka sabse bada patta. Jeetne wala agla chalta hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Bola utna banaya to bola hua number milta hai, aur har extra haath ka 0.1. Kam bana to bola hua number kat jaata hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Paanch round ke baad sabse zyada total wala jeeta.
