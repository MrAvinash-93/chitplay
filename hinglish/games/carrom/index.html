Carrom (Carrom)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Striker chalao, apni gotiyan pocket karo aur queen ko cover karo.

English name: Carrom

Kaise khelein

  1. Seat 1 safed gotiyan khelta hai aur seat 2 kaali. Har rang ki nau, aur beech mein laal rani.
  2. Striker slider se striker ko baseline pe rakho, Aim se disha aur Power se zor chuno, phir shot maaro.
  3. Apni goti pocket mein daali to phir se shot. Nahi daali to baari aage.
  4. Rani ko cover karna zaroori hai. Rani daalne ke baad usi shot ya agle shot mein apni ek goti daalo, warna rani beech mein wapas.
  5. Striker pocket mein gaya to foul: tumhari daali hui ek goti beech mein wapas aur baari khatam.
  6. Kisi ki aakhri goti tabhi andar reh sakti hai jab rani ja chuki ho.

Points kaise milte hain

Jeetne wale ko doosre ki board pe bachi har goti ka 1 point, aur rani ke 3.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo pehle apni saari gotiyan daale, rani cover karke, woh jeeta. 80 shot ho gaye to jiski kam gotiyan bachi woh jeeta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ye aam game ke rules hain, foul aur scoring thode aasaan kiye gaye hain taaki phone pe achha chale. Poore rules upar likhe hain.
