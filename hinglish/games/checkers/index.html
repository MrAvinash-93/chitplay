Checkers (Checkers)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Doosre ke mohre kood kar lo aur apne king banao.

English name: Checkers

Kaise khelein

  1. Gotiyan ek khaana tirchha aage khaali kaale khaane pe chalti hain. Goti dabao, phir nishaan wala khaana.
  2. Maarne ke liye rival goti ke upar se tirchha kood ke peeche khaali khaane pe jao. Maari hui goti hat jaati hai.
  3. Maar sakte ho to maarna zaroori hai, aur wahi goti phir kood sake to kood-ti rahegi.
  4. Aakhri line pe pahunchi goti raja ban jaati hai aur baari khatam. Raja aage aur peeche dono taraf tirchha chalta aur koodta hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Game jeet ya draw pe khatam. Result mein jeetne wale ko 1.

Kaun jeetta hai

Doosre ke paas goti ya koi chaal na bache to tum jeete. Chaalees chaal har ek bina maare ya aam goti chalaaye ho to draw.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya Checkers mein kaatna zaroori hai?

Haan. Ye rules kaatna zaroori banate hain, aur kaatne ka mauka ho to game sirf wahi chaalein deta hai.

Goti raja kaise banti hai?

Aakhri line tak pahunch ke. Taaj wali goti wahin rukti hai aur agli baari se dono taraf chal sakti hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
