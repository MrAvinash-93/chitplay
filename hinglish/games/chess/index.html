Chess (Shatranj) (Chess)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Poora game: castling, en passant, promotion aur checkmate.

English name: Chess

Kaise khelein

  1. Safed pehle chalta hai, phir baari baari. Mohra dabao, phir nishaan wala khaana.
  2. Haathi seedhi line mein chalta hai, oont tirchha, wazir dono, ghoda dhaai ghar (L shape) koodta hai aur raja ek khaana.
  3. Pyada ek khaana aage chalta hai, pehli chaal mein do bhi, aur tirchha ek khaana aage maarta hai. Aakhri line pe pahunch ke wazir, haathi, oont ya ghoda ban jaata hai.
  4. Castling: raja aur haathi na chale hon, beech ke khaane khaali hon aur raja check se na guzre, to raja haathi ki taraf do khaane chalta hai aur haathi uske upar se kood jaata hai.
  5. En passant: tumhare pyade ke bagal se rival ka pyada do khaane chale, to agli hi chaal mein use aise maar sakte ho jaise woh ek khaana chala ho.
  6. Raja pe hamla ho to woh check (shah) mein hai. Apni chaal mein check se nikalna zaroori hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Game jeet ya draw pe khatam. Result mein jeetne wale ko 1.

Kaun jeetta hai

Checkmate (shah-maat) jeetta hai: raja check mein aur bachne ka koi raasta nahi. Draw hota hai stalemate pe (koi chaal nahi, check bhi nahi), wahi position teen baar, pachaas chaal bina kaate ya pyada chalaaye, ya mohre itne kam ki maat ho hi na sake.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya ChitPlay chess mein castling aur en passant hai?

Haan, saath mein promotion, stalemate, teen baar dohraav, pachaas chaalon ka rule aur kam mohre bhi.

Computer kitna strong hai?

Ye kuch chaalein aage dekhta hai: aam players ke liye dostana opponent, koi chess engine nahi.

Mujhe kaun sa rang milega?

Host, ya table pe pehla player, safed se khelta hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
