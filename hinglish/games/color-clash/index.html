Color Clash (Color Clash)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Card games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Rang ya number milao, +2 aur Rainbow +4 chalo aur sabse pehle patte khatam karo.

English name: Color Clash

Kaise khelein

  1. Sabko saat patte milte hain. Ek patta dher shuru karta hai.
  2. Apni baari pe aisa patta chalo jo upar wale se rang, number ya nishaan mein mile.
  3. Skip (⊘) agle player ko chhod deta hai. Turn Around (⇄) disha badalta hai; do players mein yeh skip jaisa hai. +2 agle se do patte uthwaata hai aur uski baari jaati hai.
  4. Rainbow patta kisi pe bhi chal sakta hai aur agla rang tum chunte ho. Rainbow +4 bhi yahi karta hai, aur agla chaar patte uthaata hai aur baari khota hai.
  5. Chalne ko kuch nahi? Ek patta uthao. Fit ho to chal sakte ho; warna pass.

Points kaise milte hain

Jo haath khaali kare use baaki sabke haath ke patton ke points: number wale apna number, action patte 20, rainbow 50.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo pehle 150 points pahunche woh jeeta. Paanch round mein koi na pahunche to sabse zyada wala.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya Color Clash doosre rang wale card games jaisa hai?

Ye matching card games ke usi parivaar ka hai, par patte, naam aur scoring hamare apne hain.

Kitne log khel sakte hain?

Do se chaar. Khaali seatein computer bharta hai, online bhi.

Kya +2 patte jod sakte hain?

Normal game mein nahi: agla player patte uthata hai aur uski baari khatam. Game se pehle Stacking rule on karo to apna +2 ya +4 daal ke aage badha sakte ho.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
