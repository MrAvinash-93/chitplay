Mudi Hui Kahani (Consequences) (Consequences)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 3–8 players
  • Party games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Ek-ek mudi hui line se kahani likho, phir poori khol kar padho.

English name: Consequences

Kaise khelein

  1. Har player baari baari dikhaaye gaye hisse ke liye ek line likhta hai, pichhli lines dekhe bina.
  2. Doston ke saath apni lines type karo. Anjaan logon ke saath hamari likhi lines mein se chuno, taaki koi kuch bura na bheje.
  3. Saat hisson se kahani banti hai: kaun, kisse mila, kahan, kisne kya kaha, kya hua, aur duniya ne kya kaha.
  4. Phir kahani sabke padhne ke liye khulti hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Points nahi hain: sabse achhi kahani mehfil jeet leti hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Kahani poore table ko hansaaye to sab jeete.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ye naam kahan se aaya?

Ye mude kaagaz pe khela jaane wala puraana gharelu game hai, aur aakhri line "nateeja" batati hai.

Kya ajnabi mujhe kuch likh sakte hain?

Nahi. Public tables pe sab hamari tay list se hi lines chunte hain.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
