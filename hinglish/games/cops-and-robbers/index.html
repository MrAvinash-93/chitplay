Hinglish guide

Chaar chit, ek chor. Police ko chehra padh kar chor pakadna hai. Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi bhi kehte hain.

English names: Raja Rani Chor Police · Cops and Robbers

Kaise khelein

  1. Chaar chit mod kar phenki jaati hain: Raja (1000), Rani (800), Police (500) aur Chor (0).
  2. Har player ek chit uthaa kar chupchaap padhta hai.
  3. Police batata hai ki woh police hai aur use baaki teen mein se chor dhoondhna hai.
  4. Police ek naam leta hai, phir sab apni chit kholte hain.
  5. Sahi andaaza: police ke 500 bane rehte hain aur chor ko 0. Galat andaaza: chor police ke 500 le jaata hai. Raja aur Rani ke points hamesha bane rehte hain.
  6. Jitne chaho utne round khelo; sabse zyada total wala jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya Raja Rani Chor Sipahi aur Raja Rani Chor Police ek hi game hai?

Haan. Sipahi police waale ka hi purana Hindi-Urdu shabd hai, isliye Raja Rani Chor Sipahi aur Raja Rani Chor Police ek hi game ke do naam hain: wahi chaar chit, wahi points, wahi rules. Kai gharon mein Mantri bhi jodte hain, tab ise Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi kehte hain.

Kya Raja Rani Chor Police aur Cops and Robbers ek hi game hai?

Haan. Yeh wohi chaar chit wala game hai. India mein Raja Rani Chor Police ya Chor Sipahi, Pakistan mein Raja Wazir Chor Sipahi, Nepal mein Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi, USA aur UK mein Cops and Robbers kehte hain. ChitPlay ise tumhari chuni bhasha ke naam se khilata hai.

Kitne players chahiye?

Chaar chit hoti hain, isliye chaar players. ChitPlay mein khaali seatein computer bharta hai, isliye tum akele ya ek se teen doston ke saath khel sakte ho.

Har chit ke kitne points hain?

Raja 1000, Rani 800, Police 500 aur Chor 0. Chor pakadne pe police ke 500 bane rehte hain; galat andaaze pe woh 500 chor ko milte hain.

House rules kya hain?

Doosre deshon ke twist jinhe tum on kar sakte ho: andaaze se pehle ek begunaah ka pata chalna (Japan), aadhe points pe doosra mauka (USA), galat andaaze pe 200 ki saza (UK) aur pakde gaye chor pe 200 ka jurmana (China).

Kya main doston ke saath online khel sakta hoon?

Haan. Nearby Play se room banao aur 6 digit ka code, QR code ya link bhejo, ya Play Online dabake duniya bhar ke players ke saath baitho.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
