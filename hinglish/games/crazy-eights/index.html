Crazy Eights (Crazy Eights)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Card games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Rang ya number milao, aath se rang badlo aur pehle patte khatam karo.

English name: Crazy Eights

Kaise khelein

  1. Do players ho to har ek ko saat patte, zyada ho to paanch. Ek patta dher shuru karta hai.
  2. Apni baari pe aisa patta chalo jo upar wale se rang ya number mein mile.
  3. Aath kisi pe bhi chal sakta hai, aur agla rang tum bolte ho.
  4. Chalne ko kuch nahi to ek patta uthao. Chal sake to chalo; nahi to pass.
  5. Kisi ke patte khatam to round khatam.

Points kaise milte hain

Haath mein bache patte tumhare khilaaf ginte hain: aath ke 50, badshah, begum ya gulaam ke 10, ikka 1, aur baaki apna number.

Kaun jeetta hai

Teen round ke baad sabse kam penalty wala jeeta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Aath ka patta kya karta hai?

Ye kisi bhi patte pe chal sakta hai, aur chalne wala agla suit chunta hai.

Uthaane ki gaddi khatam ho jaaye to?

Chale hue patte, upar wale ko chhod ke, phent ke nayi gaddi banate hain.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
