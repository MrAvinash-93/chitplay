Dots and Boxes (Dots and Boxes)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Lines kheencho, dabbe band karo aur har baar ek aur baari pao.

English name: Dots and Boxes

Kaise khelein

  1. Baari baari do paas wale dots ke beech ek line kheencho.
  2. Tumhari line ne dabbe ki chauthi line poori ki to dabba tumhara, aur tumhe phir line kheenchni hai.
  3. Saari lines khinch gayi to game khatam.

Points kaise milte hain

Har band kiya dabba ek point.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jiske sabse zyada dabbe woh jeeta. Sab barabar to draw.

Kitne players khel sakte hain?

Do se chaar, aur jitni seatein chaho computer bhar deta hai.

Board kitna bada hai?

Paanch guna paanch dabbe, lagbhag paanch se das minute ka game.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
