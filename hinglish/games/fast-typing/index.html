Fast Typing (Fast Typing)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Ek shabd aata hai: use bilkul waisa hi, jitna tez ho sake type karo. Das shabd, har ek pichhle se lamba.

English name: Fast Typing

Kaise khelein

  1. Ek second ke liye "Get ready" dikhta hai, phir shabd aata hai.
  2. Letters waale keypad (ya keyboard) pe shabd type karo aur ✓ dabao. Har shabd pe ek hi koshish milti hai.
  3. Shabd ke neeche ki patti bacha time dikhati hai: chaar second aur har akshar ka ek second.
  4. Har phone apne player ka time usi pal se naapta hai jab shabd us pe dikha, isliye slow internet tumhari speed nahi ghatata.

Points kaise milte hain

Sahi shabd pe har second mein type kiye har akshar ke das points: chhe akshar do second mein yaani 30. Har sahi shabd pe kam se kam 5, zyada se zyada 60. Ek bhi galat akshar to us shabd ke koi points nahi. Medal 120, 200, 280 aur 360 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Das shabdon ke baad sabse zyada points waala jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya ye typing speed test hai?

Haan. Aakhir mein sahi shabdon pe tumhari average speed (akshar per second) dikhti hai, taaki tum dekh sako ki kitna sudhar rahe ho.

Shabd kis bhasha mein hote hain?

Roz ke English words, jaise animals, fruits aur ghar ki cheezein, chaar se das akshar tak lambe.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
