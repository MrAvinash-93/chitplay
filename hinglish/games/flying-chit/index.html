Flying Chit (Flying Chit)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Pankh wali chit ko classroom mein choodiyon ke beech udao, combo banao aur naye 3D styles unlock karne ke liye coins kamao.

English name: Flying Chit

Kaise khelein

  1. Stick ko drag karo, ya arrow keys, W A S D ya gamepad se chit ko upar, neeche, left aur right ghumao.
  2. Ek ring dikhata hai ki chit agli chudi ko kahan se paar karegi: sahi nishane pe ho to woh hari ho jaati hai. Chudi ke paas chit apne aap uske beech ki taraf khinchti hai.
  3. Sunehri chudi se nikal ke thodi udaan wapas pao. Score ke neeche wali bar batati hai kitni bachi hai.
  4. Ek ke baad ek chudiyan combo banati hain: ×2, ×3, ×5 tak points har ek.
  5. Chudi chhooti to combo khatam. Pankha ya kitabon ka dher lagne se bahut udaan kam hoti hai.
  6. Udaan khatam hui to chit fisal ke zameen pe aa jaati hai aur tumhari flight khatam.
  7. Har naya level ek badi sunehri chudi se shuru hota hai: 10 points aur poori udaan.

Points kaise milte hain

Har chudi apna combo deti hai: 1, phir 2, 3, 4 aur 5 points jab tak chain chale. Medal 50, 200, 500 aur 1000 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Akele ho to apna best score todo. Doosron ke saath sab ek saath udte hain aur sabse zyada score jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya Flying Chit kabhi khatam hota hai?

Nahi. Chudiyan chhoti aur kamra tez hota jaata hai, isliye hamesha ek behtar score baaki rehta hai.

Kya main alag-alag kaagaz ki chit khol sakta hoon?

Haan. Khel ke coins kamao aur Game Styles mein Blueprint Glider, Golden Origami, Neon Comet ya Rose Kite kholo. Ye sirf 3D look badalte hain, udaan nahi.

Kya do log ek screen pe khel sakte hain?

Haan. Single Phone chuno: ek player W A S D aur doosra arrow keys chalaye, ya dono gamepad lein.

Kya ye doosre udne wale games jaisa hai?

Ye hamara apna idea hai: tum 3D mein chit ko khul ke ghumate ho aur chudiyon se nikal ke use hawa mein rakhte ho, tap karke pankh nahi maarte.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
