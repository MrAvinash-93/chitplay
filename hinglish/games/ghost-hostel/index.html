Ghost Hostel (Ghost Hostel)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Bhootiya hostel mein ek raat. So kar coins kamao, darwaza mazboot karo aur subah tak tiko.

English name: Ghost Hostel

Kaise khelein

  1. Tumhara bistar har second sikke kamata hai. Achha bistar zyada kamata hai.
  2. Arrow ya pad se Bed, Door, Fan ya Fix chuno, aur Buy dabao.
  3. Door: zyada taakat, aur nayi taakat tak theek ho jaata hai. Fix: darwaze ka kuch hissa theek karta hai, phir agle fix se pehle kuch second lagte hain.
  4. Bhoot darwaze pe ho to pankhe use hawa maarte hain. Kaafi chot lagi to woh apne kone mein wapas tair jaata hai.
  5. Bhoot har tees second mein taakatwar hota hai, aur aakhri chaalees second mein aur gussa. Darwaza toota to tum pakde gaye aur bahar.

Points kaise milte hain

Bistar ke kamaye saare sikke tumhare score mein, subah tak tikne ke 100 aur bhoot ko hamesha ke liye udaane ke 150 aur.

Kaun jeetta hai

Subah tak jo apne kamre mein hai woh jeetta hai. Bhoot ko poora uda diya to raat jaldi khatam. Sab pakde gaye to bhoot jeet gaya.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ek game kitna lamba hai?

Ek raat: dhai minute.

Kya main doston ke saath khel sakta hoon?

Haan, chaar tak, har koi apne kamre mein. Khaali kamre computer players le lete hain.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
