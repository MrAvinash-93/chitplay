Hoop Flick (Hoop Flick)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–2 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Kheencho, chhodo aur ball ko agle hoop mein daalo. Lagaatar swish se combo.

English name: Hoop Flick

Kaise khelein

  1. Stick ko gulel ki tarah peeche kheencho: ball ulti taraf udegi. Jitna kheencho, utna zor se.
  2. Phenkne ke liye stick chhod do. Keys se: left aur right arrow se nishana ghumao, upar-neeche se zor, aur Space se phenko.
  3. Ball ko agle hoop mein upar se girao.
  4. Har hoop ke paas baarah second hain: laal bar batati hai ki time khatam ho raha hai.
  5. Chooka to ball court se bahar: run khatam.
  6. Har paanch basket pe naya level: hilte hoop, phir hawa, phir dono.

Points kaise milte hain

Rim chhoo ke gayi basket ka 1 point. Saaf swish ke 2, aur lagaataar swish pe zyada: har ek 5 tak. Medal 20, 50, 100 aur 200 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Akele ho to apna best todo. Dost ke saath har koi court ke apne aadhe hisse mein khelta hai aur zyada score jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya ye khatam hota hai?

Nahi. Jab tak nishana na chooke, hoop aate rehte hain.

Kya do log khel sakte hain?

Haan, ek screen pe, paas mein ya online. Har player ka court ka apna aadha hissa hota hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
