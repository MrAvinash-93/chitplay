Hungry Snake (Hungry Snake)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Samose khao, lambe hote jao aur kisi cheez se mat takrao.

English name: Hungry Snake

Kaise khelein

  1. Apne saanp ko upar, neeche, left ya right mod do. Woh kabhi rukta nahi.
  2. Snack khao, point pao aur lambe ho jao.
  3. Deewar, khud se ya doosre saanp se takraye to game khatam.
  4. Doosron ke saath jo saanp aakhir tak chale use 3 bonus points.

Points kaise milte hain

Har snack ka ek point, aur aakhir tak bache rehne ke 3.

Kaun jeetta hai

Akele ho to apna best todo. Doosron ke saath sabse zyada score jeetta hai.

Kya ye mushkil hota jaata hai?

Haan. Har chhe samose pe naya level: saanp tez hote hain aur maidan mein eent ki deewar banti hai.

Kitne log khel sakte hain?

Chaar tak, khaali seatein computer bharta hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
