Chhupan Chupai (Lapa Chupi)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Maidan mein chhupo ya sabko dhoondo. Jhaadi aur bench ke peeche chhupkar time khatam hone tak bacho.

English name: Lapa Chupi

Kaise khelein

  1. Har match mein alag player dhoondhne wala banta hai. Woh deewar ke paas aankhein band karke rukta hai aur baaki sabko chhupne ke liye paanch second milte hain.
  2. Joystick ya arrow keys se 3D maidan mein ghoomo. Sprint dabake tez bhaago, par second bhar mein saans phool jaati hai.
  3. Jhaadi, bench ya kisi cheez ke paas ruk ke hilna band karo, tab chhupoge. Hilna, ya dhoondhne wale ko paas aane dena, tumhe pakdwa dega.
  4. Sabko dhoondh liya to dhoondhne wala jeeta. Time khatam hone tak jo chhupe rahe, woh saath mein jeete.
  5. Online room mein chhupe players ki jagah doosron se chhupaayi jaati hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Dhoondhne wale ko har mile hider ka ek point, aur sabko dhoondhe to jeet. Time khatam hone tak chhupe hiders saath jeette hain aur kitne bhi finds se zyada score paate hain.

Kaun jeetta hai

Computer players, ek screen pe dost, paas mein ya online khelo. Khaali seat bot bharte hain.

Kya main akele khel sakta hoon?

Haan. Baaki seatein computer players bhar dete hain.

Kya online players dekh sakte hain ki main kahan chhupa hoon?

Nahi. Chhupe player ki jagah doosre players ke game mein bheji hi nahi jaati.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
