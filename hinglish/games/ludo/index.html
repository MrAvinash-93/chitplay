Ludo (Ludo)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Chaaron gotiyan ghar pahunchao. Chhakka aane par niklo aur doosron ko wapas bhejo.

English name: Ludo

Kaise khelein

  1. Apni baari pe Roll dabao. Goti ghar se bahar nikaalne ke liye chhakka chahiye.
  2. Chamakti goti ya uska button dabao, jitna dice aaya utna aage chalegi. Ek hi goti chal sakti ho tab bhi use tum khud dabaoge; koi goti na chal sake to baari khatam.
  3. Rival ki goti pe utro, woh wapas ghar. Start aur star wale khaane safe hain: wahan koi nahi kat sakta.
  4. Board ka chakkar laga ke goti apni home line mein mudti hai. Ghar pahunchne ke liye exact number chahiye.
  5. Chhakka, goti kaatna aur goti ghar pahunchana, har ek pe ek aur chance. Lagaatar teen chhakke aaye to baari gayi.

Points kaise milte hain

Khelne se pehle mode chuno. Normal mode mein points nahi: result mein dikhta hai kisne kitni gotiyan ghar pahunchaayi. Score mode mein har khaana 1, goti kaatna 20, goti ghar 30, aur sabse pehle chaaron khatam karne ka bonus 50.

Kaun jeetta hai

Normal mode: jo pehle chaaron gotiyan ghar le aaye woh jeeta. Score mode: 15 round ke baad, ya jab koi chaaron gotiyan ghar le aaye, sabse zyada points wala jeeta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Score mode kya hai?

Khelne ka chhota tareeka: har khaane, har katai aur har ghar pahunchi goti ke points milte hain, aur 15 round ke baad sabse zyada points wala jeetta hai, chahe saari gotiyan ghar na pahunchi hon.

Kya Ludo shuru karne ke liye chhakka chahiye?

Haan. ChitPlay mein goti chhakka aane pe hi ghar se nikalti hai, jo sabse aam rule hai, aur chhakke pe ek aur baari milti hai.

Lagaataar teen chhakke aayein to kya hota hai?

Tumhari baari wahin khatam ho jaati hai aur teesra chhakka nahi chala jaata.

Kya do players Ludo khel sakte hain?

Haan. Do players aamne-saamne ke kono mein baithte hain, taaki dono ki doori baraabar rahe.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
