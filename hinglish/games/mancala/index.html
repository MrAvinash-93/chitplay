Mancala (Mancala)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Beej bote chalo, pakdo aur apna bhandaar bharo.

English name: Mancala

Kaise khelein

  1. Har player ke paas apni taraf chhe gaddhe aur right side pe ek godaam hai. Apna koi bhara gaddha dabao.
  2. Us gaddhe ke saare beej ek ek karke ulti ghadi ki disha mein agle gaddhon mein daale jaate hain. Apne godaam mein ek daalte ho, par doosre ka godaam chhod dete ho.
  3. Aakhri beej apne godaam mein gira to phir se baari.
  4. Aakhri beej apni taraf ke khaali gaddhe mein gira, aur saamne wale gaddhe mein beej hain, to woh saare aur tumhara beej tumhare.
  5. Ek taraf ke beej khatam to doosra player apni taraf ke bache beej rakh leta hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Godaam ka har beej ek point.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jiske godaam mein sabse zyada beej woh jeeta. Barabar ho to draw.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kitne beej hote hain?

Adtaalees: baarah gaddhon mein chaar-chaar.

Kya Mancala khelne ka yahi ek tareeka hai?

Nahi, iske saikdon paaramparik roop hain. Ye sabse zyada khele jaane walon mein se ek hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
