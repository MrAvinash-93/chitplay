Nine Men's Morris (Nine Men’s Morris)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Teen ki line bana kar mohre lo. Bahut purana strategy game.

English name: Nine Men’s Morris

Kaise khelein

  1. Rakhna: baari baari kisi khaali point pe goti rakho, jab tak dono nau nau na rakh dein.
  2. Chalna: phir baari baari goti ko line ke saath paas ke khaali point pe khiskao.
  3. Jab bhi teen ki nayi line (mill) bane, rival ki ek goti hatao. Mill ki gotiyan safe hain, jab tak saari mill mein na hon.
  4. Udna: sirf teen gotiyan bachi to tumhari gotiyan kisi bhi khaali point pe kood sakti hain.

Points kaise milte hain

Game jeet ya draw pe khatam. Result mein jeetne wale ko 1.

Kaun jeetta hai

Doosre ki do gotiyan bachein ya koi chaal na ho to tum jeete. Pachaas chaal har ek bina maare ho to draw.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ise Nine Men's Morris kyun kehte hain?

Har player ke paas nau gotiyan ("men") hoti hain. "Morris" shayad gotiyon ke ek puraane naam se aaya hai.

Udna kya hai?

Jis player ke paas teen gotiyan bachein, wo kisi bhi khaali jagah pe goti le ja sakta hai, sirf paas wali pe nahi.
