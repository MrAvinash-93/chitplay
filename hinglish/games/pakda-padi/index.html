Pakdam Pakdai (Pakda Padi)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Maidan mein tag ki race. Pakadne par tag aage jaata hai; time khatam hone par kam IT rehne wala jeetta hai.

English name: Pakda Padi

Kaise khelein

  1. Joystick ya arrow keys se maidan mein ghoomo. Sprint se thodi der tez bhaago; second bhar mein saans phool jaati hai aur thoda chalna padta hai.
  2. IT wala teen tak ginta hai, phir sabko pakadta hai. Paas jaake chhoo lo to baari aage.
  3. Chhua gaya to palat ke kisi aur ko pakdo. Wapas nahi: do second tak jisne abhi chhua, use nahi chhoo sakte.
  4. Time khatam hone pe jo sabse kam der IT raha, woh jeeta. Barabari bhi ho sakti hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Jo sabse kam der IT raha woh jeeta. Kitni baar chhua, scoreboard pe ginta hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Computer players, ek screen pe dost, paas mein ya online khelo. Khaali seat bot bharte hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kitne players khel sakte hain?

Do se chaar, khaali seatein computer bharta hai.

Kaun jeetta hai?

Timer khatam hone pe jisne sabse kam time "IT" bankar bitaaya.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
