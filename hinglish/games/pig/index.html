Pig (dice) (Pig)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Dice games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Phenkte raho ya points bacha lo, par 1 aaya to poori baari gayi.

English name: Pig

Kaise khelein

  1. Apni baari pe dice phenko. 2 se 6 tak jo aaye, is baari ke total mein judta hai.
  2. Jitni baar chaho phir phenko, ya hold karke is baari ka total bank karo.
  3. 1 aaya to is baari ka total gaya aur baari aage.

Points kaise milte hain

Sirf bank kiye points tumhare score mein ginte hain.

Kaun jeetta hai

Target pe pahuncho (Quick mein 50, Classic aur Two dice mein 100), phir baaki sabko tumhe peeche chhodne ki ek aakhri baari milti hai. Uske baad sabse zyada bank wala jeeta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ise Pig kyun kehte hain?

Pakka koi nahi jaanta. Ye game kam se kam 1940 ke dashak se isi naam se khela ja raha hai.

Ek game kitna lamba hai?

Lagbhag paanch se das minute.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
