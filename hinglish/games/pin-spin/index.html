Pin Spin (Pin Spin)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Ghoomte corkboard mein pin lagao, baaki pins ko chhue bina.

English name: Pin Spin

Kaise khelein

  1. Screen tap karo, Pin button, Space ya Enter, ya gamepad button dabao: pin seedha upar board ke neeche ghus jaati hai.
  2. Gap ka intezaar karo: tumhari pin pehle se lagi pin se takrai to run khatam.
  3. Level poora karne ke liye screen ke neeche dikhi saari pins lagao.
  4. Sunehra star sticker pin karo to 5 bonus points.
  5. Har paanchva level bada board hai: woh speed aur disha badalta hai.
  6. Har level ki time limit hai (neeche patli bar): time khatam hone se pehle saari pins lagao.

Points kaise milte hain

Har lagi pin ka 1, har star ka 5, aur level poora karne pe level number ka double. Medal 25, 60, 120 aur 250 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Akele ho to apna best todo. Doosron ke saath sab apne apne board pe ek saath pin lagate hain aur sabse zyada score jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya ye kabhi khatam hota hai?

Nahi: level aate rehte hain, aur jab tak koi pin doosri pin ko na chhoo le, game chalta hai.

Kya dost saath khel sakte hain?

Haan, chaar tak, har kisi ka apna board, ek screen pe, paas mein ya online.

Kya ye har umar ke liye safe hai?

Haan. Ye push-pin aur corkboard ka timing game hai: kisi pe kuch nukeela nahi phenka jaata, aur points ki koi keemat nahi.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
