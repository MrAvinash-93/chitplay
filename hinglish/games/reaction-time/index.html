Reaction Time (Reaction Time)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Hara hone ka intezaar karo, phir jitna jaldi ho sake tap karo. Doston mein sabse tez kaun?

English name: Reaction Time

Kaise khelein

  1. Screen laal rahe tab tak ruko. Hari hote hi kahin bhi tap karo (ya Space, Enter ya gamepad button dabao), jitna jaldi ho sake.
  2. Hare se pehle, ya round 3 aur 4 ke neele nakli signal pe tap karna galat shuruaat hai aur us round mein kuch nahi milta.
  3. Tumhara time tumhare apne phone pe naapa jaata hai, screen pe hara dikhne se tap tak, isliye slow internet tumhe slow nahi banata. 100 ms se kam ka time andaaza maana jaata hai.
  4. Paanch round hote hain. Har round ke baad sabka time dikhta hai, aur table pe sabse tez ko 50 bonus points milte hain.

Points kaise milte hain

Har round mein 500 mein se tumhara time (milliseconds) ghatta hai: 250 ms pe 250 points. Galat shuruaat aur chhoote round ke 0. Medal 600, 900, 1100 aur 1300 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Paanch round ke baad sabse zyada points wala jeetta hai. Akele ho to apna best todo aur medal kamao.

Achha reaction time kitna hota hai?

Zyada log dekhi hui cheez pe lagbhag 250 se 300 milliseconds mein react karte hain. 200 ms se kam bahut tez hai.

Kya online khelna fair hai?

Haan. Har phone apne player ka time usi screen pe hara dikhne se naapta hai, isliye network ki der kisi ka time nahi badalti.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
