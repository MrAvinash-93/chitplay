Reversi (Reversi)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Doosre rang ko apne beech gher kar palto aur apna banao.

English name: Reversi

Kaise khelein

  1. Game beech mein chaar gotiyon se shuru hota hai. Kaala pehle chalta hai.
  2. Nishaan wale khaane pe goti rakho. Chaal mein kam se kam ek rival goti nayi goti aur tumhari doosri goti ke beech seedhi line mein phansni chahiye.
  3. Phansi hui saari gotiyan tumhare rang mein palat jaati hain, har disha mein ek saath.
  4. Koi chaal nahi to tumhari baari chhoot jaati hai. Dono na chal sakein to game khatam.

Points kaise milte hain

Tumhara score aakhir mein tumhare rang ki gotiyon ki ginti hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Aakhir mein jiski zyada gotiyan woh jeeta. Barabar ho to draw.

  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
