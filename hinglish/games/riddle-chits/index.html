Paheli Chits (Riddle Chits)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–10 players
  • Party games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Paheli kholo aur jawaab chuno. Hint lene par ek point katta hai.

English name: Riddle Chits

Kaise khelein

  1. Chit pe ek paheli khulti hai jo sab padhte hain.
  2. Jo jawab sahi lage woh chuno. Jab tak sab jawab na de dein, koi tumhara jawab nahi dekhta.
  3. Pakka nahi? Jawab se pehle hint dabao. Usse us paheli ke points kam ho jaate hain.
  4. Phir jawab aur sabki choice dikhti hai, aur agli chit khulti hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Sahi jawab ke 2 points, hint li to 1. Galat jawab ka kuch nahi.

Kaun jeetta hai

Aakhri paheli ke baad sabse zyada points wala jeeta.

Kya paheliyan har umar ke liye hain?

Haan. Ye puraani, parivaar ke saath boojhne wali paheliyan hain.

Kya do log khel sakte hain?

Haan, aur computer players table badi kar sakte hain.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
