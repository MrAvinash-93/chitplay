Road Dash (Road Dash)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Kabhi na khatam hone wali sadak par auto-rickshaw. Gaay aur bus se bacho, coins pakdo.

English name: Road Dash

Kaise khelein

  1. Left ya right dabao (stick drag karo, A aur D, ya arrow keys) lane badalne ke liye.
  2. Gaayon, gaddhon, buson aur doosre rickshaw se bacho.
  3. Extra points ke liye sikke uthao.
  4. Jitni der tiko sadak utni tez, aur lagbhag har 100 points pe naya level naye traffic ke saath. Ek takkar aur run khatam.

Points kaise milte hain

Jitna chalo utne points, aur har sikke ke 5.

Kaun jeetta hai

Akele ho to apna best score todo. Doosron ke saath sabse zyada score jeetta hai.

Kya sadak kabhi khatam hoti hai?

Nahi. Speed badhti rehti hai aur har level naya traffic laata hai: buses, phir do-do ek saath, phir saamne se aate auto.

Kya dost mujhse race kar sakte hain?

Haan, ek hi sadak pe chaar tak, ek screen pe ya apne-apne phone pe.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
