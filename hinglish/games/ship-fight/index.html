Ship Fight (Ship Fight)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Do jahaz, lagaatar chalti topein. Bacho, nishana lagao aur dost ka jahaz dubao.

English name: Ship Fight

Kaise khelein

  1. Apna jahaz samandar ke apne aadhe hisse mein kahin bhi le jao. Uski top har second khud doosre jahaz pe fire karti hai.
  2. Har jahaz paanch goliyan jhel sakta hai. Chattanein goliyan rokti hain, unke peeche chhupo.
  3. Beech mein dibbe aate hain: dil jahaz theek karta hai, aur top wala dibba paanch triple shot deta hai.
  4. Round bahut lamba chala to toofan jahazon ko ghisne lagta hai, pehle us jahaz ko jiski health kam hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Doosra jahaz duba do, round tumhara.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo pehle teen round jeete, match uska.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Do log kaise khelein?

Ek keyboard pe ek player W A S D aur Space, doosra arrows aur Enter chalaye. Do gamepad bhi chalte hain.

Kya main akele khel sakta hoon?

Haan, ek computer player ke khilaaf jo insaanon ki tarah galtiyan bhi karta hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
