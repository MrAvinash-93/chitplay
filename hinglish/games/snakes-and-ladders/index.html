Saanp Seedhi (Snakes and Ladders)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Board games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Seedhiyan chadho, saanpon se bacho aur theek 100 par pahuncho.

English name: Snakes and Ladders

Kaise khelein

  1. Sab board ke bahar, khaana 1 se pehle shuru karte hain. Baari baari dice phenko.
  2. Jitna dice aaya utna aage chalo, board pe number ke hisaab se upar.
  3. Seedhi ke neeche utre to upar chadh jao. Saanp ke munh pe utre to uski poonch tak neeche.
  4. Chhakka aaye to ek aur chance.
  5. Khaana 100 pe exact number chahiye. Dice zyada aaya to goti wahin rukti hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Khel ke dauraan points nahi. Result mein dikhta hai kaun kahan tak pahuncha.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo pehle theek 100 pe pahunche, woh jeeta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya theek 100 pe hi pahunchna zaroori hai?

Haan. Paasa zyada aaye to tum wahin rukte ho aur agli baari phir koshish karte ho.

Kya ye poora kismat ka game hai?

Haan, poori tarah. Isliye har umar aur har hunar ka player jeet sakta hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
