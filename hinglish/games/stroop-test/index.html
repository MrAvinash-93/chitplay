Stroop Test (Stroop Test)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Likha hai RED, syahi neeli: syahi ka rang chuno. Focus aur speed ka dimaagi game.

English name: Stroop Test

Kaise khelein

  1. Card pe ek rang ka naam rangeen syahi mein likha aata hai. Syahi ke rang wala button dabao (ya us pe dikhi arrow key).
  2. Card 6 se kuch card pe "WORD chuno" likha hota hai: tab jo shabd kehta hai wahi chuno.
  3. Har card pe sirf tumhara pehla tap gina jaata hai, isliye pakka hoke dabao. Card 11 se 15 mein time kam milta hai.
  4. Tumhara time tumhare apne phone pe card dikhne ke pal se naapa jaata hai, isliye door ke doston se online khelna bhi fair hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Sahi jawab jitna tez, utne zyada points: 2000 mein se tumhara time (milliseconds) ghata ke das se bhaag, kam se kam 10. Galat ya koi jawab nahi to 0. Doston ke saath har card pe sabse tez sahi jawab ko 20 aur. Medal 600, 1200, 1700 aur 2100 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Pandrah card ke baad sabse zyada points wala jeetta hai. Akele ho to apna best todo aur medal kamao.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Stroop test kya hai?

Psychology ka ek mashhoor dhyaan test: jab shabd aur syahi ka rang alag ho to syahi ka rang batana dheema hota hai, kyunki padhna apne aap ho jaata hai.

Kya isse dimaag tez hota hai?

Ye dhyaan ki mazedaar practice hai. Khelne se is game mein tumhara score badhta hai; ye koi medical test nahi hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
