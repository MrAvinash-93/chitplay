Zero Kata (Tic-Tac-Toe) (Tic-Tac-Toe)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Teen ek line mein. Ek minute mein seekho, har baar naya match.

English name: Tic-Tac-Toe

Kaise khelein

  1. Ek player ✕ hai aur doosra ◯. ✕ pehle chalta hai.
  2. Baari baari ek khaali khaana lo.
  3. Apne teen nishaan line mein, aade, khade ya tirchhe, aur jeet.

Points kaise milte hain

Game jeet ya draw pe khatam. Result mein jeetne wale ko 1.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo pehle teen ki line banaye woh jeeta. Nau khaane bhar gaye aur line nahi bani to draw.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya Tic-Tac-Toe hamesha jeeta ja sakta hai?

Nahi. Dono achha khelein to har game draw hota hai.

Kya computer ko haraaya ja sakta hai?

Haan. Wo aksar achha khelta hai par kabhi-kabhi chookta hai, isliye mauke pe nazar rakho.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
