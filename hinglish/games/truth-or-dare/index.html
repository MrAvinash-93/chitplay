Truth or Dare (Sach ya Himmat) (Truth or Dare)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–10 players
  • Party games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Har umar ke liye mazedaar sawaal aur challenges. Koi bhi skip kar sakte ho.

English name: Truth or Dare

Kaise khelein

  1. Apni baari pe Truth ya Dare chuno.
  2. Sawaal padho, phir zor se jawab do ya kaam karo.
  3. Point ke liye Done dabao, ya Skip dabao. Skip karne ki koi saza nahi.
  4. Baari agle player ki.

Points kaise milte hain

Har sach bataane aur har dare karne ka 1 point. Skip ka kuch nahi jaata.

Kaun jeetta hai

Aakhri baari ke baad sabse zyada points wala jeeta, waise asli jeet toh hansi hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya sawaal sabke liye theek hain?

Haan. Ye maasoom sawaal aur halki challenges hain, jaise nakal utaarna, tongue twister ya chhota dance.

Kya main koi sawaal chhod sakta hoon?

Haan, hamesha, aur isse kabhi points nahi katte.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
