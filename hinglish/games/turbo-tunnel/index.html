Turbo Tunnel (Turbo Tunnel)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Arcade games (TV par, 3D mein)
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Gaadi ko kabhi na khatam hone wali surang ke andar daudao. Traffic, laser aur girte pattharon se bacho, nitro lo.

English name: Turbo Tunnel

Kaise khelein

  1. Stick ko left ya right drag karo, ya A aur D, arrow keys ya gamepad se gaadi ko tunnel ke andar gol ghumao.
  2. Cone, barrier, gaadi, truck aur patthar chhoo liya to run khatam. Jis cheez se tumhari gaadi takraane wali hai, uske neeche laal roshni chamakti hai.
  3. Tunnel ki har line mein hamesha ek saaf raasta hota hai jahan tum time pe pahunch sakte ho. Sadak pe halke hare teer use dikhate hain.
  4. Coin ke 10 points. Neela nitro pad teen second tak distance ke points double karta hai. Hari shield ek takkar khud jhel leti hai.
  5. Tel se run khatam nahi hota, par gaadi fisalti hai aur thodi der steering dheere maanti hai.
  6. Kisi cheez ke bilkul paas se nikle to close call: 5 bonus points.

Points kaise milte hain

Jitna chalao utne points (nitro pe double), har coin ke 10 aur har close call ke 5. Medal 100, 300, 700 aur 1500 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Akele ho to apna best score todo. Doston ke saath sab ek hi tunnel mein race karte hain: jo gaadi aakhir tak chalti rahe woh jeetti hai, aur sab takra jaayein to sabse zyada score jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya ye mushkil hai?

Ye dheere aur chaude raaston se shuru hota hai. Har line mein hamesha nikalne ka raasta hota hai, isliye takkar kabhi badkismati nahi hoti.

Kya main doston se race kar sakta hoon?

Haan. Ek hi tunnel mein chaar gaadiyan: ek screen pe, Nearby se apne-apne phone pe, ya online.

Kya kuch download karna padega?

Nahi. Ye phone, tablet ya computer ke browser mein free chalta hai.

Kya ye doosre tunnel games jaisa hai?

Ye hamara apna idea hai: asli gaadiyan aur traffic poori tunnel ke andar gol ghoomte hain, aur har level mein naya khatra aata hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
