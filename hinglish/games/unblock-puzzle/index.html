Unblock Puzzle (Unblock Puzzle)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Bhari parking mein phansa ek auto-rickshaw. Cars aur trucks khisakao aur use gate se bahar nikaalo.

English name: Unblock Puzzle

Kaise khelein

  1. Har parking mein rickshaw (hara, peeli chhat), aas-paas ki gaadiyan aur uski line ke daayein taraf gate dikhta hai.
  2. Kisi gaadi ko ungli ya mouse se kheench ke khisakao. Jitna kheencho utni jaati hai, raaste ki pehli rukawat tak.
  3. Ya arrows se gaadi chuno, Hold dabao aur arrows se khisakao. Chhodne ke liye phir Hold dabao.
  4. Jab rickshaw aur gate ke beech kuch na bache, use daayein khisakao aur woh bahar nikal jaata hai. Har parking ke liye do minute hain.

Points kaise milte hain

Bahar nikalne pe 50 points, aur kam chaalon mein karne pe 50 tak aur: sabse kam chaalon se har ek zyada chaal pe 5 kam. Doston ke saath har parking se pehle nikalne waale ko 20 aur. Medal 200, 300, 400 aur 470 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Paanch parking ke baad sabse zyada points waala jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya har parking hal ho sakti hai?

Haan. Har parking ko daalne se pehle computer ne hal kiya hai, aur sabse kam chaalein tumhari chaalon ke upar dikhti hain.

Ek chaal kise kehte hain?

Ek gaadi ko khisakana, chahe kitni bhi door jaaye, ek chaal hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
