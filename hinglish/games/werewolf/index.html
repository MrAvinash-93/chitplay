Werewolf Night (Werewolf Night)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 5–10 players
  • Party games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Secret roles, shaant raatein aur shor bhari subah. Bhediyon ko pakdo.

English name: Werewolf Night

Kaise khelein

  1. Sab chupke se apna secret role padhte hain: Werewolf, Seer, Doctor ya Villager.
  2. Raat: werewolves ek player ko pakadne ke liye chunte hain, Seer kisi ka role jaanne ke liye ek player chunta hai, aur Doctor ek player ko bachaata hai.
  3. Subah: gaon ko pata chalta hai raat mein kaun pakda gaya, agar Doctor ne bachaaya na ho.
  4. Din: chat mein baat karo, phir jo bache hain woh us player ko vote dete hain jo unhe werewolf lagta hai. Sabse zyada vote wala bahar aur uska role dikhta hai. Barabar vote to koi bahar nahi.
  5. Phir raat aati hai, aur game tab tak chalta hai jab tak ek taraf na jeete.

Points kaise milte hain

Jeetne wali taraf ke sab jeette hain: werewolves saath, ya Seer, Doctor aur Villagers saath. Nau ya zyada players mein do werewolf hote hain.

Kaun jeetta hai

Saare werewolf vote se bahar ho gaye to gaon jeeta. Werewolf baaki sabke barabar ho gaye to werewolf jeete.

Kitne players chahiye?

Kam se kam paanch. Khaali seatein computer bharta hai, isliye tum akele, ek phone pe doston ke saath, paas mein ya online khel sakte ho.

Kya kisi ko sach mein chot lagti hai?

Nahi. Pakde jaane ka matlab bas itna hai ki tum baaki game baith ke dekhte ho.

Kya Doctor khud ko bacha sakta hai?

Haan, game mein bache kisi bhi player ko, khud ko bhi.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
