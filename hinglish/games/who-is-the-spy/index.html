Jasoos Kaun? (Who Is the Spy) (Who Is the Spy?)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 4–10 players
  • Party games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Sabke paas ek secret word hai, jasoos ke paas nahi. Ishaaron se use pehchano.

English name: Who Is the Spy?

Kaise khelein

  1. Har player chupke se apna secret word padhta hai.
  2. Table pe ghoomte hue har player apne word ke liye ek clue card chalta hai.
  3. Clue padho, phir sab chupke se us player ko vote do jis pe shak hai.
  4. Sabse zyada vote wale pe ilzaam. Vote barabar to kisi pe nahi, aur jasoos bach nikalta hai.
  5. Jasoos pakda gaya to use chaar options mein se doosron ka word ek baar guess karne ka mauka milta hai.

Points kaise milte hain

Bach nikla jasoos 3 pata hai. Pakda gaya jasoos sahi word guess kare to 2; galat ho to baaki sabko 1. Aath ya zyada players mein do jasoos hote hain.

Kaun jeetta hai

Teen round ke baad sabse zyada points wala jeeta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya spy ko pata hota hai ki wo spy hai?

Nahi. Spy ke paas bas ek alag shabd hota hai, aur use doosron ke ishaaron se samajhna padta hai.

Kitne players chahiye?

Kam se kam chaar. Seatein computer players bhar sakte hain.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
