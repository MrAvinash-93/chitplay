Word Guess (Word Guess)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 1–4 players
  • Pen aur paper games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Ek chhupa shabd, ek ishaara aur har baar ek akshar. Jaldi pehchano to zyada points, par koshish sirf ek.

English name: Word Guess

Kaise khelein

  1. Tumhe ek ishaara (jaise Fruit ya Sport) aur khaali tiles mein shabd dikhta hai, jismein ek akshar khula hota hai.
  2. Har dedh second mein ek aur akshar khulta hai. Aakhri akshar kabhi nahi khulta, isliye andaaza lagana hi padta hai.
  3. Letters waale keypad (ya keyboard) pe poora shabd type karo aur ✓ dabao. Har shabd pe ek hi koshish milti hai.
  4. Har phone apne player ka time khud naapta hai, aur doston ke phone pe sirf khule hue akshar jaate hain, poora shabd kabhi nahi.

Points kaise milte hain

Sahi jawab pe 10 points, aur bhejte waqt jitne akshar chhupe the har ek ke 10 aur. Doston ke saath har shabd ka sabse tez sahi jawab 10 points zyada paata hai. Galat jawab pe us shabd ke koi points nahi. Medal 80, 160, 240 aur 320 points pe.

Kaun jeetta hai

Aath shabdon ke baad sabse zyada points waala jeetta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Shabd kis bhasha mein hote hain?

Shabd roz ke English words hain, jaise fruits, animals aur ghar ki cheezein, isliye ye game English vocabulary ki achhi practice bhi hai.

Galat andaaza lagaya to kya hoga?

Us shabd ke koi points nahi milte aur tum agle shabd ka intezaar karte ho. Har shabd pe ek hi koshish hai, isliye bahut jaldi andaaza lagana risk hai.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
