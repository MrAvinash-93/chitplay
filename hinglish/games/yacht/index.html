Yacht (dice) (Yacht)

▶ Abhi free khelo

  • 👥 2–4 players
  • Dice games
  • 🆓

Hinglish guide

Paanch paase, teen baar phenko, baarah khaane. Samajhdaari se score chuno.

English name: Yacht

Kaise khelein

  1. Paanchon dice phenko. Jo rakhne hain unhe dabao, baaki phir phenko. Ek baari mein teen baar tak.
  2. Kisi bhi phenk ke baad ruk ke kisi aisi category mein score likho jo abhi use nahi hui.
  3. Ones se Sixes us number ka total dete hain. Choice saare dice ka total.
  4. Four of a kind un chaar dice ka total. Full house (teen ek jaise, do doosre jaise) paanchon ka total.
  5. Little straight (1–5) aur Big straight (2–6) ke 30. Yacht (paanchon ek jaise) ke 50.
  6. Kuch fit na ho tab bhi koi category chunni hi padegi, chahe 0 mile.

Points kaise milte hain

Tumhara total saari baarah categories ka jod hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Har ek ki baarah baari ke baad sabse zyada total jeetta hai.
