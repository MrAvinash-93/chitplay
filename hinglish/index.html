ChitPlay: dosto ke saath khelne wale 45+ free indoor games

ChitPlay ek 3D kamra hai jismein 45 se zyada indoor games hain, aur sab aapke browser mein free chalte hain. Yahan bachpan wala kaagaz ki chit ka game Raja Rani Chor Police hai, aur saath mein Ludo, Carrom, Chess, Saanp Seedhi, Call Break jaise card games, party games aur TV par chalne wale arcade games bhi.

Na app download karna, na account banana. Link kholo, apna naam chuno aur khelna shuru: akele computer players ke saath, poori duniya ke logon ke saath online, ya paas baithe dosto ke saath unke apne phone par. Har umar ke liye, bina asli paise ke.

Khelne ke tareeke

  • Guest ban kar khelo: Koi sign-up ya login nahi. Naam chuno aur turant khelo.
  • Computer ke saath: Jab koi saath na ho, computer players khaali jagah bhar dete hain. Easy, Normal ya Hard chuno.
  • Online khelo: Duniya bhar ke players ke saath ek public table par baitho.
  • Dosto ke saath Nearby: Ek phone par game shuru karo aur code ya QR code share karo. Dost apne phone se jud jaate hain.
  • Ek hi phone par: Phone baari-baari sabko do. Poori family ke liye ek device kaafi hai.
