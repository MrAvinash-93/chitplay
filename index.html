ChitPlay: indoor games online, for every age

ChitPlay is a 3D living room full of games: 47 of them, free in your browser. Play as a guest with no sign-up, get matched with players online, play the computer, or invite friends nearby with a code.

Every game here is a full game with real rules, made to play well on a phone: board games families have played for generations, card games, party games for a group of friends, quick arcade games and brain teasers. Each one plays in 3D (or in 2D, straight from above), against the computer, with strangers online, on one phone passed round, or with friends on their own phones.

Play with friends

Start a party with Play with Friends and share the code, the QR or the link. Once your friends are in, whatever game anyone starts, the whole party plays it together, on their own phones, near or far.
