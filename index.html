ChitPlay: indoor games online, for every age

ChitPlay is a 3D living room full of games: 34 of them, free in your browser. Play as a guest with no sign-up, get matched with players online, play the computer, or invite friends nearby with a code.

ChitPlay is the 4-player paper chit game, free in your browser for players of every age. Draw a folded chit, keep a straight face, and let the Police (or Sipahi, Wazir, Sheriff…) catch the Thief.

The same game has many names: Raja Rani Chor Police and Raja Rani Chor Sipahi in India, Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi in Nepal, Raja Wazir Chor Sipahi in Pakistan, Chor Police in Bangladesh, Mafia in Korea and Russia, Cops and Robbers in the USA and UK, Räuber und Gendarm in Germany. ChitPlay has all of them, each with its own rules.
