Indoor games for four people

Stuck indoors with three other people and nothing to do? The chit game needs no board and no batteries: one page, one pen, and a straight face. It has been played in homes for generations because a grandparent, a parent and a teenager can all sit down and be equally good at it.

Why it works at home

Rounds last about a minute, so it fits between other things. Nobody has to be good at anything except keeping a straight face, which makes it fair across ages. And since a different person is the guesser each round, nobody sits out.

What you need

Tear a page into four slips. Write King, Queen, Police and Thief on them (or your own version of the names), fold them and shuffle. One person tosses them on the floor and everyone picks one. Keep a score on the back of the same page.

When there are fewer than four of you

Open CHITPLAY on a phone: computer players take the empty seats. Friends in the same room can join from their own phones by scanning a QR code, and each person keeps their chit private on their own screen.

A forfeit makes it better

Agree before you start what last place has to do: sing a line of an old song, tell an embarrassing story, or make the next round of tea. CHITPLAY suggests a harmless forfeit at the end of every match.

Questions

What is the best indoor game for 4 people?

If you have no board game at hand, the chit game (known as Raja Rani Chor Police, Mafia, or Cops and Robbers) is hard to beat: it needs only paper, takes a minute to learn and suits every age.

What indoor games can adults and children play together?

Games that reward bluffing rather than skill or strength. In the chit game a child can fool an adult as easily as the other way round.

Which indoor games need no equipment?

The chit game needs just a page and a pen, or only a phone if you play it online for free.
